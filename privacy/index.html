Privacy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Open Practice Tests requires no account. You do not need to provide personal information to study or practice here.

Your saved progress

Your answers, test results, guide progress and appearance preferences are saved in your browser on your device. This saved data is not sent to Open Practice Tests or synced between devices. Clearing this site’s browser data removes it. Downloaded reports stay where you save them.

Site analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand which pages are being used and improve the site. It does not use cookies, fingerprint individual visitors, or create advertising profiles. Your browser sends Cloudflare the page address, the page you came from, your browser type and your IP address. Your answers, scores and saved progress are not sent to analytics.

External video and affiliate links

The SAT test builder links to a video on YouTube. The site does not embed a player or contact YouTube until you follow that link. YouTube’s privacy practices apply when you visit it.

The SAT prep resources page includes clearly labelled paid Amazon links. If you follow one, Amazon may attribute qualifying purchases to this site’s Associate account. We do not send your PDF, answers, scores or saved progress through these links.

After you follow an Amazon link, Amazon and its advertising partners may collect information about your visit, serve advertisements, and set or recognize cookies under their own privacy policies. You can manage cookies in your browser and adjust advertising preferences on Amazon. This site does not load Amazon advertising scripts or set Amazon cookies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Contact and other websites

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message, and use them to respond or address your feedback. Links to other websites are subject to those sites’ privacy practices.

For privacy questions, please use the contact page.

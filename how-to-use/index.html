How to use

How to use this site

Open Practice Tests is free. No account is needed.

Find your way around

Use the Practice and Study guides menus or the SAT hub to find practice tests and Reading and Writing or Math study guides.

What's available now?

Choose a test from Practice tests. Start opens the timed runner; Browse lets you move through every module and show or reset each solution without changing your timed attempt.

Read the directions before each module, then press Start Module 1 or Start Module 2. Reading directions does not use your module time, including when you continue to Math after the break.

Select an answer, or type an integer, decimal, or fraction for numeric questions. Invalid numeric entries show a format message; correct answers stay hidden until the test is complete. Use the question grid, flags, highlighting and answer elimination to review your work.

Pause stops the timer, as on the original site. Your current answers and remaining time save in this browser. Submitting locks that module. After Reading and Writing, the combined test recommends a 10-minute break with a countdown. Press Start Math when you’re ready, or end the break early. Math’s timer starts only when you choose to begin.

After completion, review your raw score and answers or download an interactive HTML report. These are practice results, not an SAT scaled score. Save a report before clearing browser storage.

Read the Lines and Angles section, or work through the Information and Ideas diagnostic one question at a time. The diagnostic has no timer or score: select an answer, use Show solution to review it, and reset individual questions to try again. Use Check all attempted questions at the bottom to reveal all your attempted solutions and color the question grid by correctness. Unanswered questions stay unrevealed. Reading and Writing and Math guide practice save your answers, crossed-out choices, checked solutions, and question order on this device when browser storage is available. Find saved guide sets in My practice. Choose Resume practice when you return, or Restart practice to clear that set. The review summary shows which questions to revisit and links back to relevant lessons. Explore all six Reading and Writing guides for teaching, worked examples, and practice.

Choose a comfortable theme

Open Appearance in the header (inside Menu on mobile) to choose Soft, Light, or Dark. Your choice is saved in this browser when browser storage is available. With a keyboard, Tab to the theme control and use the arrow keys to change themes.

Questions or feedback?

Visit Contact to email us. The Copy button copies the address when your browser supports it. You can also select and copy the address yourself.

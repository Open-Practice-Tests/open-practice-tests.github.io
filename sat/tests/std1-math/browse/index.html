Math · Module 1 · Question 1 of 22

For x>0x > 0, which expression is equivalent to x53\sqrt[3]{x^5}?

A radical becomes a fractional exponent whose numerator is the power under the radical and whose denominator is the index of the root, so x53=x5/3\sqrt[3]{x^5} = x^{5/3}. Reading the fraction upside down gives x3/5x^{3/5}, which is the cube root's fifth power turned inside out. Adding the index to the power instead of writing it beneath gives x8/3x^{8/3}, and multiplying the two gives x15x^{15}, which is the result of raising to the third power rather than taking a third root.

The sum of four consecutive odd integers is 7272. What is the greatest of the four integers?

Consecutive odd integers rise by 22, so calling the least of them nn gives n+(n+2)+(n+4)+(n+6)=4n+12=72n + (n + 2) + (n + 4) + (n + 6) = 4n + 12 = 72, and n=15n = 15. The four are 1515, 1717, 1919, and 2121, so the greatest is 2121. Answering 1515 reports the least instead of the greatest, and 1919 the third. The average, 72÷4=1872 \div 4 = 18, is not one of them at all, since four consecutive odd integers have an even number midway between the middle two.

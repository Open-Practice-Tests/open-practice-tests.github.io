Math · Module 2 · Question 18 of 22

The inequality kx + 6 ≤ 2x + 15 must hold for every real x in the interval −2 ≤ x ≤ 5. What is the greatest possible value of k?

Answer: B. 19/5 Rewrite as (k − 2)x ≤ 9. At x = 5, a necessary condition is 5(k − 2) ≤ 9, so k ≤ 19/5. This bound works: for k = 19/5, the left side is (9/5)x, whose largest value on the interval occurs at x = 5 and equals 9. Hence the greatest possible k is 19/5.

Watch for: A condition that must hold for every input is controlled by the worst input, not by a single arbitrary test value.

A nonzero real number r satisfies r − 1/r = 3. What is r4 + 1/r4?

Math · Module 2 · Question 20 of 22

A precision wire feeder advances at a constant rate for 8 seconds, then at a rate 25% faster for another 12 seconds. It advances 138 centimeters in all. How far, in centimeters, did it advance in the first 8 seconds?

Answer: B.

Let r be the initial rate in centimeters per second. The second rate is 1.25r, so 8r+12(1.25r)=1388r+12(1.25r)=138. This simplifies to 23r = 138, giving r = 6. The first-stage distance is 8r = 48.

Check: The second stage advances 12(7.5)=9012(7.5)=90 centimeters; 48 + 90 = 138.

A: This is the initial rate, not the first-stage distance.

C: This assumes the same rate throughout and allocates by time only.

D: This is the second-stage distance.

Watch for: A percentage increase changes the rate, not the second stage’s duration or the entire distance.

Math · Module 2 · Question 22 of 22

The equation x2 + (p − 1)x + p = 0 has two real roots r and s satisfying r2 + s2 = 5. What is p?

Answer: B. 2 − 22 The root sum is 1 − p and the product is p. Thus (1 − p)2 − 2p = 5, which simplifies to (p − 2)2 = 8. The candidates are p = 2 ± 22. For either candidate, the discriminant simplifies to 5 − 2p. At p = 2 + 22, this is 1 − 42 < 0, contradicting real roots. At p = 2 − 22, it is 1 + 42 > 0, so that candidate works.

Watch for: An algebraic condition on a proposed root sum and product does not by itself guarantee real roots.

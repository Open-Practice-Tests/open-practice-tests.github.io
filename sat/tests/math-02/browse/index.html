Math · Module 1 · Question 1 of 22

Which expression is equivalent to (x + 4)2 − (x − 4)2 + 3x?

Answer: B. 19x Use a difference of squares: [(x + 4) − (x − 4)][(x + 4) + (x − 4)] = 8(2x) = 16x. Adding 3x gives 19x. Direct expansion gives the same result, but requires more terms.

Watch for: The constant square terms cancel; the middle terms add rather than cancel.

Math · Module 1 · Question 3 of 22

Two adjacent interior angles of a parallelogram measure (7x + 8)° and (5x + 16)°. What is the positive difference between their measures, in degrees?

Answer: B. 18 Adjacent angles in a parallelogram are supplementary, so 7x + 8 + 5x + 16 = 180. This gives 12x = 156 and x = 13. The angles measure 99° and 81°, whose difference is 18°.

Watch for: Adjacent angles are supplementary; it is opposite angles that are equal.

Math · Module 1 · Question 4 of 22

Quantity A is 80% of quantity B, where B > 0. Quantity A is what percent less than the average of A and B?

Answer: A. 1009% Let B = 100, so A = 80 and their average is 90. The difference from that average to A is 10. Relative to the average, the percent decrease is (10/90)(100)% = 100/9%.

Watch for: The comparison base is the average, not B or A.

Math · Module 1 · Question 5 of 22

The variables R, s, and t satisfy R = 4(s − t)5 + t. Which expression equals s?

Answer: D. 5R − t4 Multiply by 5: 5R = 4s − 4t + 5t = 4s + t. Subtract t and divide by 4 to obtain s = (5R − t)/4.

Watch for: The final t in the original equation is outside the fraction and becomes 5t after multiplying by 5.

Math · Module 1 · Question 6 of 22

An exponential function g(x) = abx, with a > 0 and b > 0, satisfies g(2) = 45 and g(5) = 360. What is g(4)?

Answer: A. 180 The output ratio over three input units is 360/45 = 8, so b3 = 8 and b = 2. Moving from input 2 to input 4 doubles the output twice: g(4) = 45(22) = 180.

Watch for: The ratio 8 is a three-step multiplier, not a one-step multiplier.

Math · Module 1 · Question 7 of 22

A line passes through (−3, 11) and has a y-intercept of 5. What is the x-coordinate of its x-intercept?

Answer: D. 5/2 Using (0, 5) and (−3, 11) gives slope (11 − 5)/(−3 − 0) = −2. The equation is y = −2x + 5. At the x-intercept, y = 0, so x = 5/2.

Watch for: The x-intercept is found by setting the output to zero, not by reading the constant directly.

Math · Module 1 · Question 8 of 22

The histogram represents ten integer measurements. Each interval includes its left endpoint but not its right endpoint. What is the greatest possible mean of the ten measurements?

Answer: B. 39 The three bins contain 3, 4, and 3 measurements. To maximize the mean, place every measurement at the largest integer in its bin: 29, 39, or 49. The greatest possible sum is 3(29) + 4(39) + 3(49) = 390, so the greatest mean is 39.

Watch for: The right endpoints 30, 40, and 50 belong to the next intervals and cannot be used as values in these bins.

Math · Module 1 · Question 9 of 22

For all x ≠ 2, −1, 3x − 2 + 4x + 1 = 7x + c(x − 2)(x + 1). What is the value of c?

Answer: B. −5 Use the common denominator. The combined numerator is 3(x + 1) + 4(x − 2) = 3x + 3 + 4x − 8 = 7x − 5. Thus c = −5. The stated exclusions preserve the domain of both fractions.

Watch for: Multiply each numerator by the factor missing from its own denominator.

Math · Module 1 · Question 10 of 22

A sector of a circle has central angle 2π/3 radians and arc length 10π. What is the area of the sector?

Answer: A. 75π Using arc length s = rθ, the radius is r = (10π)/(2π/3) = 15. The sector is one-third of the circle, so its area is (1/3)π(15)2 = 75π. Equivalently, use 12 rs.

Watch for: The angle is already in radians; do not divide it by 360.

Math · Module 1 · Question 11 of 22

An order costs $18 plus $4.50 per notebook. Orders of at least 10 notebooks receive a $12 discount on the total. With a budget of $70, what is the greatest number of notebooks that can be ordered?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 14 For an order with at least 10 notebooks, the cost is 18 + 4.50n − 12 = 6 + 4.50n. The budget gives n ≤ 64/4.50 = 14.222 . . ., so the largest whole number is 14. It qualifies for the discount and costs $69; 15 would cost $73.50. Orders below 10 cannot beat 14.

Watch for: Check that the candidate qualifies for the discount before using the discounted formula.

Math · Module 1 · Question 12 of 22

Let f (x) = (x2 − 4)(x − 5) and g(x) = f (x + 1). What is the sum of the positive zeros of g?

Answer: C. 5 The zeros of f are −2, 2, and 5. Solve x + 1 = −2, 2, 5 to obtain the zeros −3, 1, and 4 of g. Only 1 and 4 are positive, so their sum is 5.

Watch for: Do not include the negative zero, and subtract 1 from the old inputs rather than adding it.

Math · Module 1 · Question 14 of 22

A laboratory performs two calibration procedures. Three runs of procedure A and two runs of procedure B take 146 minutes in total. Five runs of A and three runs of B take 237 minutes. Each procedure always takes the same amount of time. How many more minutes does one run of A take than one run of B?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 17 Let a and b be the run times. The equations are 3a + 2b = 146 and 5a + 3b = 237. Multiply the first by 3 and the second by 2: 9a + 6b = 438 and 10a + 6b = 474. Subtract to obtain a = 36. Then 108 + 2b = 146, so b = 19. The difference is 17.

Watch for: The totals refer to multiple runs; do not compare the totals directly.

Math · Module 1 · Question 15 of 22

A quadratic function has the form f (x) = ax2 + bx + c. If f (1) + f (3) = 18 and f (2) = 5, what is a?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 4 Expand the given outputs: f (1) + f (3) = 10a + 4b + 2c = 18, while 2 f (2) = 8a + 4b + 2c = 10. Subtracting eliminates b and c, leaving 2a = 8. Therefore a = 4.

Watch for: Two aggregate conditions can determine a particular coefficient even though they do not determine the whole function.

Math · Module 1 · Question 16 of 22

The line y = mx + 8 is translated 6 units to the right and 3 units upward. The translated line passes through (10, 5). What is the x-coordinate of the original line's x-intercept?

Answer: C. 16/3 Undo the translation: the point (10, 5) came from (4, 2) on the original line. Thus 2 = 4m + 8, so m = −3/2. The original equation is y = −32 x + 8. Setting y = 0 gives x = 16/3.

Watch for: Undo both coordinate shifts before substituting into the original equation.

Math · Module 1 · Question 17 of 22

Researchers recruited 120 volunteers and randomly assigned each to solve the same puzzle using layout A or layout B. Conditions other than layout were kept the same. The analysis found a difference in average completion time unlikely to be explained by chance assignment alone. Which conclusion is best supported?

Answer: C. The experiment provides evidence that layout affected average completion time for these participants under the tested conditions. Random assignment helps isolate the effect of layout within the experiment. The stated analysis supports an effect on average completion time under these conditions. Because the volunteers were not randomly sampled from all adults, broad population generalization is not established by the design alone.

Watch for: Random assignment supports a causal comparison; it does not make a volunteer group representative of every adult.

Math · Module 1 · Question 18 of 22

The line ax + by = 1 contains the points (2, 3) and (4, 1). What is a + b?

Answer: D. 2/5 The points give 2a + 3b = 1 and 4a + b = 1. Subtracting yields 2a − 2b = 0, so a = b. Then 5a = 1, and a = b = 1/5. Their sum is 2/5.

Watch for: The unknowns here are the coefficients a and b, not the point coordinates.

Math · Module 1 · Question 19 of 22

An isosceles right triangle has perimeter 12 + 122. What is its area?

Answer: C. 36 If each leg is s, the hypotenuse is s2, so s(2 + 2) = 12 + 122. The value s = 62 satisfies this equation. Its area is 12 s2 = 12(72) = 36. Equivalently, rationalize the expression for s.

Watch for: The perimeter includes both equal legs and the hypotenuse; do not treat the given radical expression as a side length.

Math · Module 1 · Question 20 of 22

The function p is defined by p(x) = (x − 3)2 + k, where k < 0. If p(1)p(5) = 49, what is p(0)?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: −2 Inputs 1 and 5 are equally distant from 3, so both outputs equal 4 + k. Thus (4 + k)2 = 49, giving k = 3 or k = −11. The condition k < 0 selects −11. Therefore p(0) = 9 − 11 = −2.

Watch for: A square equation has two sign possibilities; the sign condition on k selects the valid one.

Math · Module 1 · Question 21 of 22

The unique solution of 2x + ay = 9 and 3x + 4y = 15 satisfies x = y + 1. What is a?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 25/12 Substitute x = y + 1 into the second equation: 3(y + 1) + 4y = 15, so y = 12/7 and x = 19/7. The first equation becomes 38/7 + 12a/7 = 9, giving 12a = 25. Thus a = 25/12. At this value, the two equations do not have proportional variable coefficients, so the solution is unique.

Watch for: The extra condition is a relationship between the solution coordinates, not a third independent choice of point.

Math · Module 1 · Question 22 of 22

A function has the form f (x) = abx + c, where a > 0 and b > 1. If f (0) = 7, f (1) = 11, and f (2) = 19, what is f (−1)?

Answer: D. 5 The consecutive output differences are 4 and 8. They equal a(b − 1) and ab(b − 1), so their ratio is b = 2. Then a(b − 1) = 4 gives a = 4, and a + c = 7 gives c = 3. Thus f (−1) = 4(2−1) + 3 = 5.

Watch for: Because of the vertical offset, use ratios of successive differences, not ratios of the outputs themselves.

Math · Module 2 · Question 1 of 22

For x > 0, which expression is equivalent to ((x3)12x−14)4?

Answer: D. x7 Inside the parentheses, subtract exponents: x3/2/x−1/4 = x3/2+1/4 = x7/4. Raising this to the fourth power multiplies exponents, yielding x7. The positivity condition makes the fractional-power rules unambiguous.

Watch for: Dividing by a negative power adds its positive counterpart to the exponent.

Math · Module 2 · Question 2 of 22

If 3x − 27 = x + 43, what is 2x + 1?

Answer: D. 35 Cross-multiply to obtain 3(3x − 2) = 7(x + 4). Thus 9x − 6 = 7x + 28, so 2x = 34. The requested expression is 2x + 1 = 35.

Watch for: Stop at the requested expression rather than accidentally reporting x = 17.

Math · Module 2 · Question 3 of 22

A solid is enlarged by the same scale factor in every dimension. Its surface area increases by 44%, and its volume increases by 91 cubic units. What was its original volume, in cubic units?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 125 The surface-area multiplier is 1.44, so the length multiplier is 1.44 = 1.2. The volume multiplier is 1.23 = 1.728. If the original volume is V, the increase is 1.728V − V = 0.728V = 91. Therefore V = 91/0.728 = 125. The enlarged volume is 216, which is 91 more than 125.

Watch for: A 44% increase in surface area is neither a 44% increase in length nor a 44% increase in volume.

Math · Module 2 · Question 4 of 22

Every value in a combined data set belongs to group A or group B, with no overlap. Group A has mean 17, group B has mean 29, and the combined mean is 26. How many times as many values are in group B as in group A?

Answer: C. 3 Let the group counts be a and b. Then 17a + 29b = 26(a + b), so 3b = 9a and b/a = 3. The combined mean is closer to 29 because that group has more observations.

Watch for: An unweighted average of the two means would be valid only for equal group sizes.

Math · Module 2 · Question 5 of 22

Nonnegative real numbers x and y satisfy 2x + y ≤ 18 and x + y ≥ 10. What is the least possible value of 2x + 3y?

Answer: B. 22 Subtract the second bound from the first to obtain x ≤ 8. Since x + y ≥ 10, this also gives y ≥ 2. Rewrite the target as 2(x + y) + y ≥ 2(10) + 2 = 22. The point (8, 2) satisfies both original inequalities with equality and attains 22.

Watch for: Prove a lower bound and exhibit a feasible point that achieves it.

Math · Module 2 · Question 6 of 22

Real numbers p and q satisfy 4p = 8q and p + q = 10. What is 2p−q?

Answer: D. 4 Convert to base 2: 22p = 23q, so 2p = 3q. Together with p + q = 10, this gives p = 6 and q = 4. Hence 2p−q = 22 = 4.

Watch for: Equal exponential values with the same positive base different from 1 require equal exponents.

Math · Module 2 · Question 7 of 22

Points A, B, C, D lie on a circle in that order. The interior angles at B and D measure (4x + 15)° and (5x + 3)°, respectively. What is the degree measure of arc ADC?

Answer: A. 174 Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary. Thus 4x + 15 + 5x + 3 = 180, giving x = 18. Angle ABC measures 87° and intercepts arc ADC. An intercepted arc has twice its inscribed angle's measure, so the arc measures 174°.

Watch for: Arc ADC is the arc from A to C through D, which does not contain vertex B.

Math · Module 2 · Question 8 of 22

The roots of x2 + 3x − 10 = 0 are r and s. A quadratic with leading coefficient 1 has roots r + 4 and s + 4. What is its constant term?

Answer: B. −6 The original root sum is r + s = −3 and product is rs = −10. The new constant term is the new root product: (r + 4)(s + 4) = rs + 4(r + s) + 16 = −10 − 12 + 16 = −6.

Watch for: Adding 4 to each root does not add 8 to their product.

Math · Module 2 · Question 9 of 22

Which model is most consistent with the scatterplot?

Answer: B. y = 5x The points lie near (1, 5), (4, 10), (9, 15), and (16, 20). These are the values predicted by y = 5x. The data increase at a diminishing rate, rather than along a line or an accelerating quadratic or exponential curve.

Watch for: Compare predicted values across the full horizontal range, not only at the first point.

Math · Module 2 · Question 10 of 22

For what real value of a does (a2 − 9)x = a + 3 have no solution for x?

Answer: C. 3 The coefficient of x is zero when a = 3 or a = −3. At a = 3, the equation is 0 = 6, so it has no solution. At a = −3, it is 0 = 0, so every real x works. For all other a, the coefficient is nonzero and there is one solution.

Watch for: Check the right side at each value that makes the variable coefficient zero.

Math · Module 2 · Question 11 of 22

The function h is defined for all real x by h(x) = x2 − 6x + 25. What is its minimum value?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 4 Complete the square inside the radical: x2 − 6x + 25 = (x − 3)2 + 16. This expression is at least 16, attained at x = 3. Because square root is increasing on nonnegative inputs, the minimum output is 16 = 4.

Watch for: The minimum radicand is 16; the function output is its square root.

Math · Module 2 · Question 12 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (x + 5) − 2 f (x) = 11 − 3x for every real x. What is f (2)?

Answer: A. 10 Let f (x) = mx + b. Then f (x + 5) − 2 f (x) = −mx + 5m − b. Match this to 11 − 3x: m = 3 and 15 − b = 11, giving b = 4. Therefore f (2) = 3(2) + 4 = 10.

Watch for: The identity must match both the variable coefficient and the constant term.

Math · Module 2 · Question 13 of 22

In a right triangle, an acute angle θ satisfies cos θ = 3 sin θ. The area of the triangle is 12. What is the square of the hypotenuse length?

Answer: D. 80 If the opposite leg is u and the adjacent leg is v, then v/h = 3u/h, so v = 3u. The area is 12 u(3u) = 12, giving u2 = 8. By the Pythagorean theorem, h2 = u2 + (3u)2 = 10u2 = 80.

Watch for: The ratio fixes relative side lengths, not their absolute lengths; use the area to find the scale.

Math · Module 2 · Question 14 of 22

The function f is defined by f (x) = a2x + b4x. If f (0) = 7 and f (1) = 22, what is f (2)?

Answer: A. 76 The conditions give a + b = 7 and 2a + 4b = 22. Subtract twice the first equation from the second to get 2b = 8, so b = 4 and a = 3. Hence f (2) = 3(4) + 4(16) = 76.

Watch for: A sum of two exponential functions is not generally a single exponential with a constant output ratio.

Math · Module 2 · Question 16 of 22

A factory makes 60% of its units on line A and the rest on line B. Exactly 5% of line A units and 8% of line B units are rejected. Among rejected units, what fraction were made on line B?

Answer: C. 16/31 Imagine 1,000 units: 600 from A and 400 from B. Rejected counts are 0.05(600) = 30 and 0.08(400) = 32. Among the 62 rejected units, the fraction from B is 32/62 = 16/31.

Watch for: The denominator is the total rejected count, not the total number of units or all line B units.

Math · Module 2 · Question 17 of 22

The roots of 3x2 − 11x + 6 = 0 are r and s. What is 12r + 1 + 12s + 1?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 4/7 Combining fractions gives (2r + 2s + 2)/(4rs + 2r + 2s + 1). The quadratic gives r + s = 11/3 and rs = 2, so the target is (22/3 + 2)/(8 + 22/3 + 1) = (28/3)/(49/3) = 4/7. Alternatively, factor the quadratic as (3x − 2)(x − 3); its roots give reciprocals 3/7 and 1/7. Neither denominator is zero.

Watch for: The added 1 in each denominator prevents replacing the expression by 1/[2(r + s) + 2].

Math · Module 2 · Question 18 of 22

A circle has equation x2 + y2 + Dx + Ey + 45 = 0. Its center is in the first quadrant, and it is tangent to both coordinate axes. What is D + E?

Answer: A. −125 A circle tangent to both axes with first-quadrant center has center (r, r) for radius r > 0. Its equation expands to x2 + y2 − 2rx − 2ry + r2 = 0. Therefore r2 = 45, so r = 35, and D + E = −4r = −125.

Watch for: The first-quadrant condition determines the signs of the center coordinates and hence the linear coefficients.

Math · Module 2 · Question 19 of 22

Plan A normally costs $45 plus $0.12 per unit. Plan B normally costs $20 plus $0.20 per unit. A promotion reduces only plan A's fixed fee by 15% and only plan B's per-unit price by 25%. What is the least whole number of units for which promotional plan A costs less than promotional plan B?

Answer: 609 The promotional costs are 38.25 + 0.12n and 20 + 0.15n. Requiring the first to be smaller gives 18.25 < 0.03n, or n > 608.333 . . .. The least whole number is 609. Checking 608 and 609 confirms that the cheaper plan changes between those counts.

Math · Module 2 · Question 20 of 22

The quadratic function f (x) = a(x − 2)2 + b satisfies f (1) = 15 f (5) and f (4) = 20. What is b?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 4 The first condition gives a + b = (9a + b)/5, so 5a + 5b = 9a + b and b = a. The second condition gives 4a + b = 20. Thus 5a = 20, so a = b = 4. The resulting function is genuinely quadratic because a ≠ 0.

Math · Module 2 · Question 21 of 22

For exactly two real values of a, the system (a + 2)x + 4y = 6 and 6x + (a − 2)y = 9 has no solution. What is the sum of the squares of those two values of a?

Answer: B. 56 For the variable coefficients to be proportional, (a + 2)(a − 2) = 4(6), giving a2 = 28. The candidates are a = ±28. Coincident lines would also need the right-side ratio 9/6 = 3/2, which requires 6/(a + 2) = 3/2, hence a = 2. Neither candidate is 2, so both give distinct parallel lines and no solution. Their squared values sum to 28 + 28 = 56.

Watch for: A zero determinant is only the candidate condition; rule out coincident lines before counting no-solution cases.

Math · Module 2 · Question 22 of 22

What is the least integer a for which x2 + (a − 6)x + 2a = 0 has two distinct negative real roots?

Answer: C. 19 The root sum is 6 − a, so two negative roots require a > 6. Their product 2a is then positive. Distinct real roots require (a − 6)2 − 8a > 0, which factors as (a − 2)(a − 18) > 0. Combining this with a > 6 gives a > 18. Thus the least integer is 19. Negative sum and positive product ensure that both real roots are negative.

Watch for: The sign conditions alone do not ensure real roots; the discriminant condition is also necessary.

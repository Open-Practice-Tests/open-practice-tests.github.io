Math · Module 1 · Question 1 of 22

A linear model gives total cost C(x) = 42x + 54, where x is the number of items produced, measured in hundreds, and C(x) is in dollars. By how many dollars does the model's cost increase for each additional individual item?

Answer: D. 0.42 An increase of 1 in x represents 100 additional items and raises the cost by $42. Therefore each individual item raises the cost by 42/100 = 0.42 dollar. The fixed term 54 does not affect the rate.

Watch for: The input unit is one hundred items, not one item.

Math · Module 1 · Question 2 of 22

Which expression is equivalent to (x + 1)(x + 2) − (x + 1)(x − 4)?

Answer: A. 6x + 6 Factor out the common binomial: (x + 1)[(x + 2) − (x − 4)] = (x + 1)(6) = 6x + 6. The quadratic terms cancel if the products are expanded instead.

Watch for: The subtraction inside the brackets changes the sign of the −4.

Math · Module 1 · Question 3 of 22

A hall's registered attendance was 64% of its capacity. Registration then increased by 12.5%, while the hall's capacity stayed the same. The new registration is what percent of capacity?

Answer: D. 72% Multiply the original occupancy percentage by the growth factor: 64%(1.125) = 72%. Equivalently, one-eighth of 64 is 8, so the occupancy rises from 64% to 72%.

Watch for: A 12.5% increase in the count is not an increase of 12.5 percentage points in occupancy.

Math · Module 1 · Question 4 of 22

If 5 − 2(3x − 4) = 4(x + 1) − 7, what is 5x + 2?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 10 Expand both sides: 13 − 6x = 4x − 3. Hence 10x = 16, so 5x = 8. The requested value is 5x + 2 = 10. Substitution with x = 8/5 confirms the original equality.

Watch for: Distribute the negative coefficient across both terms in 3x − 4.

Math · Module 1 · Question 5 of 22

For a > 0 and b > 0, which expression is equivalent to (8a3b−2)2/3?

Answer: A. 4a2b4/3 Apply the power to every factor: 82/3 = 4, a3(2/3) = a2, and b−2(2/3) = b−4/3. Rewriting the negative power gives 4a2/b4/3.

Watch for: Multiply exponents; do not add the outer exponent to the inner exponent.

Math · Module 1 · Question 6 of 22

The line ax + 5y = 18 passes through (−4, 6). What is its slope?

Answer: C. −3/5 Substitute the point: −4a + 30 = 18, so a = 3. The line is 3x + 5y = 18, or y = −35 x + 185. Therefore its slope is −3/5.

Watch for: The coefficient a is not itself the slope of the line.

Math · Module 1 · Question 7 of 22

A data set has mean 14. When one value equal to 8 is removed, the mean of the remaining values is 14.4. How many values were in the original data set?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 16 Let the original count be n, so the original sum is 14n. After removal, (14n − 8)/(n − 1) = 14.4. Multiplying gives 14n − 8 = 14.4n − 14.4, or 0.4n = 6.4. Thus n = 16. The remaining sum is 216, and 216/15 = 14.4.

Watch for: Removing a value changes both the sum and the count.

Math · Module 1 · Question 8 of 22

An exponential function f (x) = abx, with a > 0 and b > 0, satisfies f (−1) = 12 and f (1) = 3. For what value of k is f (k) = 48?

Answer: D. −3 The ratio over two input units is 3/12 = 1/4, so b2 = 1/4 and b = 1/2. Since f (1) = a/2 = 3, a = 6. Thus 6(1/2)k = 48, or (1/2)k = 8 = 23, giving k = −3.

Watch for: For a decreasing exponential, larger outputs occur at smaller inputs.

Math · Module 1 · Question 9 of 22

A tank initially holds 80 liters. A pump removes 6 liters per minute for the first 5 minutes. After that, a second pump adds 3 liters per minute while the first pump keeps running. How many liters are in the tank 12 minutes after the first pump starts?

Answer: C. 29 After 5 minutes, the tank holds 80 − 6(5) = 50 liters. For the next 12 − 5 = 7 minutes, the net rate is 3 − 6 = −3 liters per minute. The final volume is 50 − 3(7) = 29 liters.

Watch for: The second stage lasts 7 minutes, and both pumps operate during that stage.

Math · Module 1 · Question 10 of 22

Triangles ABC and DEF are similar, with A corresponding to D, B to F, and C to E. If AB = 18, BC = 24, AC = 30, and DF = 15, what is DE?

Answer: A. 25 Side AB corresponds to DF, so the scale factor from the first triangle to the second is 15/18 = 5/6. Side AC corresponds to DE, giving DE = 30(5/6) = 25.

Watch for: Use the stated vertex correspondence rather than matching sides by alphabetical position.

Math · Module 1 · Question 11 of 22

A function has the form f (x) = x2 + bx + c. If f (2) = f (10) and f (0) = 9, what is f (6)?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: −27 The equal outputs at 2 and 10 place the symmetry axis at their midpoint, x = 6. For a monic quadratic, this gives b = −12. Also, c = f (0) = 9. Therefore f (6) = 36 − 72 + 9 = −27. Alternatively, subtract the two equal-output equations to find b.

Watch for: Equal outputs at distinct inputs identify the axis midpoint, not a root.

Math · Module 1 · Question 12 of 22

For a positive constant k, the equation kx + 8 = 3(x + 4) has a negative solution for x. Which condition gives all possible values of k?

Answer: C. 0 < k < 3 Rearrange to (k − 3)x = 4. A negative solution requires 4/(k − 3) < 0, so the denominator must be negative: k < 3. Combine this with the given k > 0 to obtain 0 < k < 3. The value k = 3 gives no solution.

Watch for: Before multiplying an inequality by an unknown expression, determine its sign.

Math · Module 1 · Question 13 of 22

Two surveys report estimates of the same population percentage. Survey A reports 48% with a margin of error of 3 percentage points. Survey B reports 55% with a margin of error of 4 percentage points. Which percentage lies in both reported intervals?

Answer: B. 51% Survey A gives the interval from 45% to 51%, and survey B gives the interval from 51% to 59%. Their only common value is 51%. This question asks only about interval membership, not whether the surveys prove a change.

Watch for: Use percentage points to form the endpoints, and include the endpoints in the stated intervals.

Math · Module 1 · Question 14 of 22

A quadratic polynomial P with leading coefficient 1 leaves remainder 3 when divided by x − 2 and remainder 15 when divided by x − 4. What is P(3)?

Answer: B. 8 Write P(x) = x2 + bx + c. The remainder statements give P(2) = 3 and P(4) = 15, so 4 + 2b + c = 3 and 16 + 4b + c = 15. Subtracting gives 12 + 2b = 12, hence b = 0 and c = −1. Therefore P(3) = 9 − 1 = 8.

Watch for: The monic condition fixes the leading coefficient; without it, the two values would not determine P(3).

Math · Module 1 · Question 15 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (1) = 8 and f (5) = −4. If g(x) = f (−x) + 6, what is g(3)?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 26 The slope of f is (−4 − 8)/(5 − 1) = −3, so f (x) = −3x + 11. Hence g(3) = f (−3) + 6 = [9 + 11] + 6 = 26.

Watch for: The negative sign acts on the input; f (−3) is not − f (3).

Math · Module 1 · Question 16 of 22

A circle has radius 8. Two radii form a 60° central angle. What is the area of the region inside the resulting minor sector but outside the triangle formed by the two radii and their chord?

Answer: C. 32π3 − 163 The sector is one-sixth of the circle, so its area is 16π(8)2 = 32π/3. The triangle has two sides of length 8 and included angle 60°, making it equilateral. Its altitude is 43, and its area is 12(8)(43) = 163. Subtract the triangle from the sector.

Watch for: The requested region is the sector minus the triangle, not the entire sector.

Math · Module 1 · Question 17 of 22

What value of x satisfies (x−4)2 = 2x − 5?

Answer: B. 3 The left side equals |x − 4|. If x ≥ 4, then x − 4 = 2x − 5 gives x = 1, which fails the branch condition. If x < 4, then 4 − x = 2x − 5 gives x = 3, which is valid. Checking the original gives 1 = 1.

Watch for: u2 = |u|, not always u.

Math · Module 1 · Question 18 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (3) = 11 and f (7) = 27. Define g(x) = 2 f (x) − f (2x). What is g(2026)?

Answer: D. −1 The slope is (27 − 11)/(7 − 3) = 4, so f (x) = 4x − 1. Then g(x) = 2(4x − 1) − (8x − 1) = −1 for every real x. Therefore g(2026) = −1; the large input does not require large arithmetic.

Watch for: The variable terms cancel, but the intercept terms do not both disappear.

Math · Module 1 · Question 19 of 22

Of 120 event passes, 44 include weekend admission and 30 include evening admission. Exactly 18 include both. A pass is selected at random from those that do not include weekend admission. What is the probability that it includes evening admission?

Answer: B. 3/19 There are 120 − 44 = 76 passes without weekend admission. Among them, 30 − 18 = 12 include evening admission but not weekend admission. Therefore the conditional probability is 12/76 = 3/19.

Watch for: Remove the overlap from the evening count, and restrict the denominator to passes without weekend admission.

Math · Module 1 · Question 20 of 22

A circle has center (h, 2) and passes through (1, 5) and (7, 5). What is the square of its diameter?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 72 The two points lie on a horizontal chord, whose perpendicular bisector is x = 4. Thus the center is (4, 2). The radius squared is (1 − 4)2 + (5 − 2)2 = 18. Since the diameter is twice the radius, its square is 4(18) = 72.

Watch for: Doubling a length multiplies its square by 4, not 2.

Math · Module 1 · Question 21 of 22

Pack A contains 4 red pieces and 3 blue pieces. Pack B contains 2 red pieces and 5 blue pieces. What is the least total number of packs needed to obtain at least 40 red pieces and at least 35 blue pieces?

Answer: A. 11 At least 40 + 35 = 75 pieces are needed, and each pack contains 7 pieces, so at least ⌈75/7⌉ = 11 packs are necessary. This lower bound is attainable: 9 packs of A and 2 of B give 4(9) + 2(2) = 40 red and 3(9) + 5(2) = 37 blue pieces. Thus the minimum is 11.

Watch for: The total-piece bound is only necessary until a combination meeting both color requirements is exhibited.

Math · Module 1 · Question 22 of 22

The roots of 2x2 − 9x + k = 0 are real numbers r and s. If r2 + s2 = 53/4, what is k?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 7 The root sum is r + s = 9/2, and the product is rs = k/2. Use r2 + s2 = (r + s)2 − 2rs to obtain 53/4 = 81/4 − k. Therefore k = (81 − 53)/4 = 7. Its discriminant is 81 − 56 = 25 > 0, consistent with real roots.

Watch for: For a nonmonic quadratic, divide the coefficient relationships by the leading coefficient.

Math · Module 2 · Question 1 of 22

Ride service A charges $7.40 plus $2.15 per kilometer. Service B charges $5.90 plus $2.45 per kilometer. For what trip length, in kilometers, are the charges equal?

Answer: B. 5 Set the charges equal: 7.40 + 2.15d = 5.90 + 2.45d. Thus 1.50 = 0.30d and d = 5. Both charges are then $18.15.

Watch for: The fixed-fee difference must be compared with the per-kilometer difference.

Math · Module 2 · Question 2 of 22

For all real x, (x − 3)(x + 7) = (x + h)2 + k, where h and k are constants. What is h − k?

Answer: A. 27 Expand the product: x2 + 4x − 21. Completing the square gives (x + 2)2 − 25. Thus h = 2, k = −25, and h − k = 2 − (−25) = 27.

Watch for: The constant outside the completed square is −25, not −21.

Math · Module 2 · Question 3 of 22

A robot moves 54 meters at 0.6 meter per second, stops for 30 seconds, and then moves 36 meters at 0.9 meter per second. What is its average speed for the entire trip, including the stop, in meters per minute?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 33.75 The moving times are 54/0.6 = 90 seconds and 36/0.9 = 40 seconds. Total elapsed time is 90 + 30 + 40 = 160 seconds, or 8/3 minutes. Total distance is 90 meters, so the average speed is 90/(8/3) = 33.75 meters per minute.

Watch for: Use total distance divided by total elapsed time, including the stationary interval.

Math · Module 2 · Question 4 of 22

The function f (x) = a(x + 1)2 − 8 satisfies f (2) = 10. Define g(x) = f (x) − f (0). What is g(3)?

Answer: D. 30 The condition gives 9a − 8 = 10, so a = 2. Then f (3) = 2(16) − 8 = 24 and f (0) = 2 − 8 = −6. Therefore g(3) = 24 − (−6) = 30.

Watch for: Subtracting a negative output increases the difference.

Math · Module 2 · Question 5 of 22

The line ax + 4y = 12 has positive x- and y-intercepts. Its x-intercept is greater than its y-intercept. Which condition on a is necessary and sufficient?

Answer: C. 0 < a < 4 The y-intercept is 3, and the x-intercept is 12/a. Its positivity requires a > 0. With that sign established, 12/a > 3 is equivalent to a < 4. Thus the exact condition is 0 < a < 4.

Watch for: Positivity must be established before multiplying the inequality by a.

Math · Module 2 · Question 6 of 22

What is the value of cos(17π/6)?

Answer: C. −3/2 Subtract one full revolution: 17π/6 − 2π = 5π/6. This angle is in quadrant II with reference angle π/6, where cosine has magnitude 3/2 and is negative. Thus the value is −3/2.

Watch for: Reduce the angle modulo 2π, then use the quadrant to choose the sign.

Math · Module 2 · Question 7 of 22

The line y = 3x + c passes through the vertex of the graph of y = x2 − 8x + 19. What is the y-coordinate of the other intersection of the line and the parabola?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 12 Complete the square: x2 − 8x + 19 = (x − 4)2 + 3, so the vertex is (4, 3). Because the line passes through it, 3 = 3(4) + c, giving c = −9. Equating the two expressions for y yields x2 − 11x + 28 = (x − 4)(x − 7) = 0. The other intersection has x = 7, so its y-coordinate is 3(7) − 9 = 12.

Watch for: The vertex is one intersection, but the question asks for the output at the other intersection.

Math · Module 2 · Question 8 of 22

The equations 5x + 4 = 3x − 10 and 7x + a = 2(x − a) have the same solution for x. What is a?

Answer: B. 35/3 The first equation gives 2x = −14, so x = −7. Substitute this into the second: −49 + a = 2(−7 − a) = −14 − 2a. Hence 3a = 35 and a = 35/3. The second equation has a nonzero coefficient of x, so this is its unique solution.

Watch for: Sharing a solution does not mean the equations have identical coefficients.

Math · Module 2 · Question 9 of 22

Data set A consists of two copies each of 2, 4, and 6. Data set B consists of four copies each of 2, 4, and 6. Compared with set A, set B has which mean and population standard deviation?

Answer: D. The same mean and the same population standard deviation Both sets assign one-third of their values to each of 2, 4, and 6, so both have mean 4. Replicating every value equally multiplies both the sum of squared deviations and the count by the same factor. Their ratio, the population variance, is unchanged, so the population standard deviation is unchanged.

Watch for: More observations do not automatically imply more spread. This question explicitly uses population standard deviation.

Math · Module 2 · Question 10 of 22

How many positive integer values of b make both roots of x2 + bx + 36 = 0 negative integers? Repeated roots are allowed.

Answer: A. 5 Write the roots as −u and −v for positive integers u, v. Then uv = 36 and b = u + v. The unordered factor pairs are (1, 36), (2, 18), (3, 12), (4, 9), (6, 6), giving distinct sums 37, 20, 15, 13, 12. Therefore there are five values of b.

Watch for: Do not count reversed factor pairs twice, and include the repeated-root pair because the question allows it.

Math · Module 2 · Question 11 of 22

The linear function f has slope 4 and satisfies f (2k) = 3 f (k) − 11 and f (k + 1) = 19. What is k?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 19/4 Because the slope is 4, f (k) = 19 − 4 = 15. The first condition then gives f (2k) = 3(15) − 11 = 34. For a linear function, f (2k) − f (k) = 4(2k − k) = 4k. Therefore 4k = 34 − 15 = 19, so k = 19/4.

Watch for: The change in output is the slope times the change in input, even when the input contains an unknown constant.

Math · Module 2 · Question 12 of 22

A right triangle has legs of lengths 18 and 12. A rectangle lies inside the triangle with two sides along the legs and its opposite vertex on the hypotenuse, as shown. The rectangle's horizontal side is twice its vertical side. What is its area?

Answer: D. 2592/49 Let the vertical side be h and the horizontal side be 2h. The hypotenuse joins (18, 0) and (0, 12), so a point on it satisfies x/18 + y/12 = 1. Thus 2h/18 + h/12 = 1, giving 7h/36 = 1 and h = 36/7. The area is 2h2 = 2592/49.

Watch for: The rectangle reaches the hypotenuse at one corner; its sides are not the full triangle legs.

Math · Module 2 · Question 13 of 22

For all x ≠ 4, x2 + ax + bx − 4 = x + 7 + 3x − 4. What is a + b?

Answer: A. −22 Multiply by x − 4 on the stated domain: x2 + ax + b = (x − 4)(x + 7) + 3 = x2 + 3x − 25. Matching coefficients gives a = 3 and b = −25, so their sum is −22.

Watch for: The remainder 3 is added after multiplying the quotient by the divisor.

Math · Module 2 · Question 14 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (2x + 1) + f (3x − 2) = 20x + 17 for all real x. What is f (0)?

Answer: C. 21/2 Let f (x) = mx + b. The left side becomes m(2x + 1) + b + m(3x − 2) + b = 5mx − m + 2b. Matching coefficients gives m = 4 and −4 + 2b = 17, so b = 21/2. This intercept is f (0).

Watch for: Each function evaluation contributes its own copy of the intercept.

Math · Module 2 · Question 15 of 22

A random sample of 200 objects from a collection of 7,800 contains 42 objects of type R. The resulting estimate of the population percentage of type R has a margin of error of 5 percentage points. What population count corresponds to the upper endpoint of the reported interval?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 2028 The sample percentage is 42/200 = 21%. Its upper interval endpoint is 21% + 5% = 26%. The corresponding estimated count is 0.26(7800) = 2028. This is an endpoint of an estimate interval, not a guaranteed maximum count in the collection.

Watch for: Add percentage points before converting the interval endpoint into a population count.

Math · Module 2 · Question 16 of 22

The equation 6x + k = x + 1 has x = 3 as a solution. What is its other real solution?

Answer: B. 1 Using x = 3 gives 18 + k = 4, so k = −2. Squaring the resulting equation yields 6x − 2 = (x + 1)2, or x2 − 4x + 3 = 0. The candidates are 1 and 3. Both satisfy x + 1 ≥ 0 and check in the original equation, so the other solution is 1.

Watch for: Not every squared equation produces an extraneous root; check candidates instead of discarding one automatically.

Math · Module 2 · Question 17 of 22

A rectangular prism has side lengths x, 2x, and 3x, where x > 0. Its total surface area is 792. What is its volume?

Answer: B. 1296 The surface area is 2[x(2x) + x(3x) + (2x)(3x)] = 22x2. Thus x2 = 792/22 = 36, and positivity gives x = 6. The volume is x(2x)(3x) = 6x3 = 6(216) = 1296.

Watch for: Include both faces for each pair of dimensions when computing total surface area.

Math · Module 2 · Question 18 of 22

For how many integer values of k does (k − 2)x = 3k + 1 have a unique real solution greater than 4?

Answer: D. 6 For k ≠ 2, rewrite the solution as x = 3 + 7/(k − 2). To exceed 4, we need 7/(k − 2) > 1. The denominator must be positive, so k > 2; then multiplying gives k − 2 < 7, or k < 9. The integers 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 are exactly the six possibilities.

Watch for: The sign of k − 2 controls the inequality, and k = 2 does not give a unique solution.

Math · Module 2 · Question 19 of 22

What is the sum of all real solutions of 4x − 6(2x) + 8 = 0?

Answer: A. 3 Let u = 2x, which is positive. Since 4x = u2, the equation is u2 − 6u + 8 = (u − 2)(u − 4) = 0. Thus 2x = 2 or 2x = 4, giving x = 1 or x = 2. Their sum is 3.

Watch for: The roots of the substituted quadratic are values of 2x, not values of x.

Math · Module 2 · Question 20 of 22

The line y = x + 3 intersects the circle x2 + y2 = 65 at points P and Q. What is PQ2?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 242 Substitute the line into the circle: x2 + (x + 3)2 = 65, so x2 + 3x − 28 = 0. Factoring gives x = −7 and x = 4. The points are (−7, −4) and (4, 7). The squared distance is [4 − (−7)]2 + [7 − (−4)]2 = 121 + 121 = 242.

Watch for: The difference of the x-coordinates alone is not the chord length.

Math · Module 2 · Question 21 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (2) = 3, f (5) > 12, and f (8) < 30. If f (0) is an integer, what is the sum of all possible values of f (0)?

Answer: C. −9 Write f (x) = m(x − 2) + 3. The first inequality gives 3m + 3 > 12, so m > 3. The second gives 6m + 3 < 30, so m < 4.5. Thus f (0) = 3 − 2m lies strictly between −6 and −3. Its possible integer values are −5 and −4, whose sum is −9.

Watch for: Both inequalities are strict, so the endpoint intercepts −6 and −3 are not allowed.

Math · Module 2 · Question 22 of 22

For two real values of k, the equation x2 + (k − 5)x + k2 − 4k = 0 has two real roots whose positive difference is 4. What is the sum of the squares of those values of k?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 10 For a monic quadratic, the difference between its two roots is the square root of its discriminant. Thus (k − 5)2 − 4(k2 − 4k) = 16. Simplifying gives −3k2 + 6k + 9 = 0, or (k − 3)(k + 1) = 0. The values are 3 and −1, each giving positive discriminant 16. Their squared sum is 9 + 1 = 10.

Watch for: Use the root separation, not the root sum, to form the discriminant condition.

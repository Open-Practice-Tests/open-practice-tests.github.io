Math · Module 1 · Question 1 of 22

The variables x and y satisfy 5x + 3y = 29. If x decreases by 6 while the equation remains true, by how much must y increase?

Answer: A. 10 The change in the left side must be zero. Thus 5(−6) + 3Δy = 0, so 3Δy = 30 and Δy = 10. Equivalently, the line has slope −5/3, and (−5/3)(−6) = 10.

Watch for: The two variable changes must offset after being multiplied by their coefficients.

Math · Module 1 · Question 2 of 22

Which quadratic function has no real zeros?

Answer: C. f (x) = x2 + 2x + 5 For x2 + 2x + 5, completing the square gives (x + 1)2 + 4, which is strictly positive for every real x. The other choices have discriminants 0, 16, and 24, so each has at least one real zero.

Watch for: A double root still counts as a real zero.

Math · Module 1 · Question 3 of 22

A plan uses a length scale of 1 : 250. A region on the plan has area 128 square centimeters. What is the actual area of the region, in square meters?

Answer: B. 800 The actual area is 128(250)2 = 8,000,000 square centimeters. One square meter contains 1002 = 10,000 square centimeters, so the actual area is 8,000,000/10,000 = 800 square meters.

Watch for: Square the scale factor before converting the resulting square units.

Math · Module 1 · Question 4 of 22

Real numbers x and y satisfy 2x + 5y = 41 and 3x − 2y = 14. What is x − y?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 3 Multiply the first equation by 2 and the second by 5: 4x + 10y = 82 and 15x − 10y = 70. Adding gives 19x = 152, so x = 8. Then 16 + 5y = 41, so y = 5. The requested difference is 3.

Watch for: After solving the system, return to the requested combination rather than reporting one coordinate.

Math · Module 1 · Question 5 of 22

The function f (x) = x2 − x − 56x − 8 agrees with the line y = x + 7 everywhere in its domain. Which point on that line is not on the graph of f ?

Answer: B. (8, 15) Factor the numerator as (x − 8)(x + 7). For x ≠ 8, cancellation gives f (x) = x + 7. However, the original denominator is zero at 8, so the point with line coordinates (8, 15) is absent. The other listed inputs are allowed.

Watch for: Cancellation does not restore the excluded input.

Math · Module 1 · Question 6 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (−4) + f (10) = 30. What is f (3)?

Answer: D. 15 The input 3 is the midpoint of −4 and 10. For a linear function, the output at the midpoint is the average of the endpoint outputs. Therefore f (3) = [ f (−4) + f (10)]/2 = 15. Algebraically, both sides equal 3m + b when f (x) = mx + b.

Watch for: The individual endpoint values are not needed when their sum and midpoint are known.

Math · Module 1 · Question 7 of 22

A solution of mass 240 grams contains 18% dissolved material by mass. It is mixed with 160 grams of a solution containing 6% of the same dissolved material. Then 80 grams of pure water is added. What percent of the final mixture's mass is dissolved material?

Answer: D. 11% The dissolved masses are 0.18(240) = 43.2 grams and 0.06(160) = 9.6 grams, for a total of 52.8 grams. Adding water does not change that total. The final mixture has mass 240 + 160 + 80 = 480 grams, so the percentage is 52.8/480 = 0.11 = 11%.

Watch for: Pure water changes the denominator but does not add dissolved material.

Math · Module 1 · Question 8 of 22

Positive real numbers a and b satisfy a − b = 3 and ab = 18. What is (a + b)2?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 81 Use (a + b)2 = (a − b)2 + 4ab. Substituting the given quantities gives 32 + 4(18) = 9 + 72 = 81. The pair a = 6, b = 3 also verifies consistency.

Watch for: The difference of the two squares is 4ab, not 2ab.

Math · Module 1 · Question 9 of 22

Conveyor A moves 11 packages per minute and starts 6 minutes before conveyor B, which moves 17 packages per minute. Each conveyor continues at its constant rate. How many minutes after B starts will the total numbers of packages moved by the two conveyors be equal?

Answer: C. 11 Let t measure minutes after B starts. Conveyor A has run for t + 6 minutes, so 11(t + 6) = 17t. Thus 66 = 6t and t = 11. Both conveyors have then moved 187 packages.

Watch for: The two conveyors have different elapsed operating times, even at the same clock time.

Math · Module 1 · Question 10 of 22

In triangle ABC, AB = AC. The measure of angle B is 15° less than twice the measure of angle A. What is the measure of angle C, in degrees?

Answer: B. 69 Let angle A measure a. The equal sides imply equal base angles, so B = C = 2a − 15. Using the angle sum gives a + 2(2a − 15) = 180, so 5a = 210 and a = 42. Hence C = 84 − 15 = 69.

Watch for: The equal base angles are opposite the equal sides; angle A is the vertex angle.

Math · Module 1 · Question 11 of 22

A ball's height is modeled by h(t) = −4t2 + 24t + 7 for t ≥ 0, where height is in meters and time is in seconds. What is the maximum height above its initial height, in meters?

Answer: C. 36 Complete the square: h(t) = −4(t − 3)2 + 43. The maximum height is 43 meters at t = 3, which is in the domain. The initial height is h(0) = 7 meters. The requested gain above the initial height is 43 − 7 = 36 meters.

Watch for: The maximum absolute height and the maximum increase above the initial height are different quantities.

Math · Module 1 · Question 12 of 22

A line is perpendicular to 2x + 5y = 17 and passes through the intersection of x + y = 8 and x − y = 2. What is its y-intercept?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: −19/2 Adding the system gives 2x = 10, so its intersection is (5, 3). The given line has slope −2/5, so the required slope is 5/2. In y = (5/2)x + b, substitute (5, 3): b = 3 − 25/2 = −19/2.

Watch for: Find the intersection first, and use the negative reciprocal of the original slope.

Math · Module 1 · Question 14 of 22

The graph of a quadratic function f is shown. Let g(x) = f ((x − 2)2). What is the sum of all real zeros of g?

Answer: D. 4 The graph shows that the zeros of f are −4 and 9. For g(x) = 0, the inner value (x − 2)2 must equal one of those zeros. It cannot equal −4 over the reals. Setting it equal to 9 gives x = 2 ± 3, or −1 and 5. Their sum is 4.

Watch for: A squared input can reach only nonnegative values, so not every zero of the outer function creates real zeros of the composition.

Math · Module 1 · Question 15 of 22

For real constants a and b, the equation (a + b)x + (a − b) = 0 has the unique solution x = 3. What is the solution of (a − b)x + (a + b) = 0?

Answer: A. 1/3 The first condition gives a − b = −3(a + b), with a + b ≠ 0. Thus a − b ≠ 0 as well. In the second equation, substitute to obtain −3(a + b)x + (a + b) = 0. Divide by a + b to get −3x + 1 = 0, so x = 1/3.

Watch for: The uniqueness condition permits division by the coefficient; swapping the coefficients reciprocates the root in this setup.

Math · Module 1 · Question 16 of 22

Point P has coordinates (−3/5, 4/5). Point Q is the reflection of P across the line y = x. What is PQ2?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 98/25 Reflection across y = x swaps coordinates, so Q = (4/5, −3/5). The horizontal and vertical changes are 7/5 and −7/5. Therefore PQ2 = (7/5)2 + (−7/5)2 = 98/25.

Watch for: The requested quantity is already the squared distance; no final square root is needed.

Math · Module 1 · Question 17 of 22

What is the sum of all real solutions of 1x − 2 + 1x + 2 = 34?

Answer: A. 8/3 The domain excludes x = ±2. Combine the left side to get 2x/(x2 − 4) = 3/4, so 8x = 3x2 − 12. The resulting quadratic is 3x2 − 8x − 12 = 0, with positive discriminant 208 and root sum 8/3. Neither 2 nor −2 is a root of that quadratic, so both real roots are valid.

Watch for: Preserve the denominator exclusions while using the quadratic root-sum shortcut.

Math · Module 1 · Question 18 of 22

The solution set of (2c − 1)x ≤ −12 is exactly x ≥ 4. What is c?

Answer: C. −1 The direction x ≥ 4 means the coefficient 2c − 1 must be negative. The boundary occurs at equality, so (2c − 1)(4) = −12, giving 2c − 1 = −3 and c = −1. With that value, −3x ≤ −12 indeed becomes x ≥ 4.

Watch for: Matching the boundary is not enough; verify that division reverses the inequality in the required direction.

Math · Module 1 · Question 19 of 22

Five distinct positive integers have mean 11, median 12, and greatest value 18. What is the greatest possible value of the least integer?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 5 Write the ordered values as a < b < 12 < d < 18. Their sum is 55, so a + b + d = 25. Since the values are distinct integers, b ≥ a + 1 and d ≥ 13, giving 25 ≥ 2a + 14 and a ≤ 5.5. Thus a ≤ 5. The set 5, 6, 12, 14, 18 attains a = 5 and satisfies all conditions.

Watch for: An integer upper bound must be checked with a complete valid data set.

Math · Module 1 · Question 20 of 22

In the figure, AB = AC = 13, BC = 10, and BD = 4. Point D lies on BC. What is AD2?

Answer: D. 145 Let M be the midpoint of BC. The altitude from A meets BC at M, with BM = 5. By the Pythagorean theorem, AM2 = 132 − 52 = 144. Since BD = 4, the distance DM is 1. Hence AD2 = AM2 + DM2 = 144 + 1 = 145.

Watch for: D is not the midpoint of the base, so AD is not the altitude.

Math · Module 1 · Question 21 of 22

Linear functions P and Q satisfy P(x) + 2Q(x) = 7x + 8 and 2P(x) + Q(x) = 5x + 13 for every real x. For what value of x is P(x) = 0?

Answer: B. −6 Double the second relation and subtract the first: 3P(x) = 2(5x + 13) − (7x + 8) = 3x + 18. Therefore P(x) = x + 6, which equals zero at x = −6. No separate formula for Q is needed.

Watch for: Treat P(x) and Q(x) as the two unknown quantities in a linear system.

Math · Module 1 · Question 22 of 22

What is the sum of all integer values of a for which x2 − (2a + 3)x + a2 + 3a − 4 = 0 has exactly one positive real solution?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: −5 The quadratic factors as (x − a − 4)(x − a + 1), so the distinct roots are a + 4 and a − 1. Exactly one is positive when a + 4 > 0 and a − 1 ≤ 0, giving −4 < a ≤ 1. The integer values are −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, whose sum is −5. At a = 1, the other root is zero, which is not positive.

Watch for: The boundary for a zero root must be handled carefully: include a = 1 but exclude a = −4.

Math · Module 2 · Question 1 of 22

A company's charge for n units is C(n) = F + pn, where F and p are positive constants. If C(10) = 3C(2) and C(8) = 80, what is F?

Answer: B. 16 The first condition gives F + 10p = 3F + 6p, so F = 2p. Then C(8) = 2p + 8p = 10p = 80, giving p = 8 and F = 16. Checking gives C(10) = 96 and C(2) = 32.

Watch for: Tripling an output triples the fixed fee as well as the variable part of that output.

Math · Module 2 · Question 2 of 22

A quadratic function with leading coefficient 1 has y-intercept −12 and one zero at x = −3. What is its other zero?

Answer: D. 4 For a monic quadratic, the product of the roots equals the constant term, which is the y-intercept −12. If the other zero is r, then (−3)r = −12, so r = 4. The function is (x + 3)(x − 4).

Watch for: The constant gives the product of the roots, not their sum.

Math · Module 2 · Question 3 of 22

A data set has mean 18 and population standard deviation 5. Two values a and b, with a ≥ b, are added. The mean and population standard deviation are both unchanged. What is a − b? (Population variance is the average squared deviation from the mean.)

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 10 Let the original count be n. An unchanged mean requires a + b = 36, so write a = 18 + d and b = 18 − d with d ≥ 0. The original squared deviations sum to 25n. The unchanged variance requires 25n + 2d2 = 25(n + 2), so d2 = 25 and d = 5. Thus a − b = 2d = 10.

Watch for: The squared-deviation total must grow when the count grows, even if the variance stays the same.

Math · Module 2 · Question 4 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (x + 2) − f (x) = −6 for every real x. For a real number c, f (c) = c and f (c + 4) = −2. What is c?

Answer: C. 10 The output drops 6 over every 2 input units, so the slope is −3. Therefore f (c + 4) = f (c) − 12 = c − 12. Set this equal to −2 to get c = 10.

Watch for: The condition f (c) = c links an input and output; it does not make the whole function the identity.

Math · Module 2 · Question 5 of 22

What real value of x satisfies x + 2x − 2 = 3?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 16 The domain requires x ≥ 0 and x ≠ 4. Multiply by the nonzero denominator: x + 2 = 3x − 6. Thus 2x = 8, giving x = 4 and x = 16. It checks in the original as 6/2 = 3.

Watch for: The excluded input is x = 4, where the denominator vanishes; the valid root has x = 4.

Math · Module 2 · Question 6 of 22

A right triangle is inscribed in a circle with its hypotenuse as a diameter. The circle has area 289π. The sine of one acute angle of the triangle is 8/17. What is the triangle's area?

Answer: C. 240 The radius is 17, so the hypotenuse is 34. The side opposite the given angle is (8/17)(34) = 16. The other leg is 342 − 162 = 30. Therefore the triangle's area is 12(16)(30) = 240.

Watch for: The hypotenuse is the diameter, not the radius.

Math · Module 2 · Question 7 of 22

The equation (a − 1)x + 7 = 3x + b is true when x = 2 and when x = 5. What is a + b?

Answer: D. 11 Subtract the equation at x = 2 from the equation at x = 5: 3(a − 1) = 9, so a = 4. Substituting into either equation gives b = 7. Thus a + b = 11. A linear equation true at two distinct inputs is an identity after the coefficients are matched.

Watch for: Do not interpret the wording as saying there are exactly two solutions; the equation is actually true for every real input.

Math · Module 2 · Question 8 of 22

Two quantities are modeled by P(t) = 360(1.2)t and Q(t) = 640(0.9)t. At what value of t are the quantities equal?

Answer: A. 2 Divide the equality by 360(0.9)t, which is positive. This gives (1.2/0.9)t = 640/360, or (4/3)t = 16/9 = (4/3)2. Since the base is positive and not 1, t = 2. Both quantities then equal 518.4.

Watch for: Compare the ratio of the growth factors rather than adding their percentage changes.

Math · Module 2 · Question 9 of 22

A researcher has responses from a random sample of 500 distinct residents. The researcher copies every response once, creating a file with 1,000 rows, and claims that the margin of error can now be reduced solely because the row count doubled. Which statement best evaluates the claim?

Answer: B. The claim is not justified because copying records adds no new independent observations. The file still contains information from only the original 500 sampled residents. Duplicated rows do not create a second independent sample or reduce the uncertainty caused by which residents were selected. The sample percentage also stays the same, because every record is replicated equally.

Watch for: A data-file row count is not necessarily the number of independently sampled people.

Math · Module 2 · Question 10 of 22

Plan A costs $20 for the first 5 gigabytes of data plus $6 per gigabyte beyond 5. Plan B costs $8 plus $4 per gigabyte for all data used. For usage greater than 5 gigabytes, how many gigabytes make the two costs equal?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 9 For usage u > 5, plan A costs 20+6(u −5) = 6u −10, and plan B costs 8+4u. Set them equal: 6u −10 = 8+4u, so 2u = 18 and u = 9. This lies in the required pricing interval, and both plans cost $44.

Watch for: The additional charge under plan A applies only to the amount above 5 gigabytes.

Math · Module 2 · Question 11 of 22

For what value of k does |x − 2| + |x + 4| = k have infinitely many real solutions?

Answer: C. 6 For every x between −4 and 2, the left side is (2 − x) + (x + 4) = 6. Thus k = 6 gives every point in that interval as a solution. Outside the interval, the expression equals either −2x − 2 or 2x + 2, so a fixed other k yields at most two solutions.

Watch for: The sum of distances to two fixed points is constant between them; it is not constant everywhere.

Math · Module 2 · Question 12 of 22

A circle has center (−2, 5) and passes through P = (4, 13). What is the y-intercept of the line tangent to the circle at P?

Answer: A. 16 The radius from the center to P has slope (13 − 5)/(4 − (−2)) = 8/6 = 4/3. A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency, so its slope is −3/4. In y = −34 x + b, substitute (4, 13): 13 = −3 + b, so b = 16.

Watch for: Use the negative reciprocal of the radius slope before finding the tangent's intercept.

Math · Module 2 · Question 13 of 22

Positive numbers a and b satisfy a + 2ba − b = 4. What is 3a − ba + 4b?

Answer: B. 5/6 Clear the first denominator: a + 2b = 4a − 4b, so a = 2b. Substituting into the target gives (6b − b)/(2b + 4b) = 5b/(6b) = 5/6. Positivity ensures b ≠ 0, and the recovered relationship also respects a − b ≠ 0.

Watch for: The ratio determines the relative values of a and b even though their individual sizes are not determined.

Math · Module 2 · Question 14 of 22

A nonzero real number r satisfies r − 1/r = 3. What is r4 + 1/r4?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 119 Square the given relation: r2 − 2 + 1/r2 = 9, so r2 + 1/r2 = 11. Square again: r4 + 2 + 1/r4 = 121. Therefore the requested sum is 121 − 2 = 119. Both possible real values of r give the same target.

Watch for: The cross term changes from −2 in the first square to +2 in the second.

Math · Module 2 · Question 15 of 22

Eighty sensor units are randomly assigned to firmware A or firmware B. Every unit using A is tested at 20°C, and every unit using B is tested at 30°C. Mean battery duration differs between the groups. What prevents attributing that difference to firmware alone?

Answer: D. Test temperature changes together with firmware, so their separate effects are not isolated. Random assignment helps balance initial differences between units, but the experiment changes two conditions together: firmware and temperature. A difference can reflect either condition or their combination. Testing both firmware types at the same temperature, or randomly assigning firmware within each temperature, would better isolate firmware's effect.

Watch for: Random assignment does not separate the effects of two treatment features that always occur together.

Math · Module 2 · Question 16 of 22

A quantity is modeled by G(t) = 950(1.065)t/3, where t is measured in months. Which value is closest to the time when G(t) = 1500?

Answer: B. 21.8 months Set 950(1.065)t/3 = 1500. A graphing calculator can find the intersection of y = 950(1.065)x/3 and y = 1500, giving t ≈ 21.8 months. Equivalently, t = 3 ln(1500/950)/ln(1.065). The factor 1.065 applies every three months, not every month.

Watch for: An answer near 7.3 treats the exponent as t rather than t/3.

Math · Module 2 · Question 17 of 22

For how many integer values of t does the system below have a unique solution satisfying x > 0 and y < 0? (t + 2)x + (t − 2)y = t2 + 8

(t − 2)x + (t + 2)y = t2 − 8

Answer: C. 6 Adding gives 2t(x + y) = 2t2. For t ≠ 0, this yields x + y = t. Subtracting gives 4(x − y) = 16, so x − y = 4. Thus x = (t + 4)/2 and y = (t − 4)/2, and the sign conditions give −4 < t < 4. At t = 0, the equations are dependent, so the solution is not unique. Math · Module 2 · Question 19 of 22

The equation x2 + (p − 1)x + p = 0 has two real roots r and s satisfying r2 + s2 = 5. What is p?

Answer: D. 2 − 22 The root sum is 1 − p and the product is p. Thus (1 − p)2 − 2p = 5, which simplifies to (p − 2)2 = 8. The candidates are p = 2 ± 22. For either candidate, the discriminant simplifies to 5 − 2p. At p = 2 + 22, this is 1 − 42 < 0, contradicting real roots. At p = 2 − 22, it is 1 + 42 > 0, so that candidate works.

Watch for: An algebraic condition on a proposed root sum and product does not by itself guarantee real roots.

Math · Module 2 · Question 20 of 22

The circle (x − a)2 + y2 = a2 − 12, where a > 12, is tangent to the line y = 2x. What is a2?

Answer: A. 60 Substitute y = 2x into the circle: (x − a)2 + 4x2 = a2 − 12, giving 5x2 − 2ax + 12 = 0. A tangent has exactly one intersection, so the discriminant is zero: 4a2 − 240 = 0. Hence a2 = 60, which satisfies the stated radius condition.

Watch for: The radius also depends on a; carry the full circle equation through the substitution.

Math · Module 2 · Question 21 of 22

Two boxes contain a total of 104 counters. First, one-fourth of the counters initially in box A are moved to box B. Then 10 counters are moved from B to A. The boxes now contain equal numbers of counters. How many counters were initially in box A?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 56 Let the initial count in A be a. After the first transfer, A contains 3a/4 and B contains 104 − 3a/4. After the return transfer, equality gives 3a/4 + 10 = 94 − 3a/4. Thus 3a/2 = 84, so a = 56. The sequence is (56, 48) → (42, 62) → (52, 52).

Watch for: The first transfer is a fraction of the initial count in A, not of the combined total.

Math · Module 2 · Question 22 of 22

A quadratic function f has the following properties. The line y = 3x + 2 meets its graph only at the point with x-coordinate 1, and the line y = −x + 10 meets its graph only at the point with x-coordinate 3. What is f (5)?

Answer: A. 1 Let the leading coefficient be a ≠ 0. A single intersection at x = 1 means f (x) − (3x + 2) = a(x − 1)2, so the linear coefficient of f is 3 − 2a. The other tangency gives f (x) − (−x + 10) = a(x − 3)2, so the same coefficient is −1 − 6a. Equate them to get a = −1. The first identity then gives f (x) = −x2 + 5x + 1, so f (5) = 1.

Watch for: A single intersection with a quadratic means a double root of the difference, not merely a point that lies on both graphs.

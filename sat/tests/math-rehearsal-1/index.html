Which expression is equivalent to 32 · 33?

Answer: A / 35 When multiplying powers with the same base, add exponents: 32+3 = 35. Multiplying exponents would apply to a power raised to a power.

Math · Module 1 · Question 3 of 22

What is 35% of 160?

Answer: B / 56 Convert to a decimal and multiply: 0.35(160) = 56. The percent is a fraction of the stated base 160.

Math · Module 1 · Question 4 of 22

Two adjacent angles form a straight angle. One measures 117°. What is the measure of the other angle?

Answer: C / 63° Angles forming a straight angle sum to 180°. Thus the other angle is 180 − 117 = 63°.

What is the slope of the line shown?

Answer: C / −3/2 The line passes through (0, 9) and (6, 0). Its slope is (0 − 9)/(6 − 0) = −9/6 = −3/2.

Math · Module 1 · Question 6 of 22

What is the positive solution to x2 − 4x − 12 = 0?

Answer: A / 6 Factor as (x − 6)(x + 2) = 0. The roots are 6 and −2, so the positive root is 6.

Math · Module 1 · Question 7 of 22

A delivery cost, in dollars, is modeled by C(d) = 18 + 2.5d, where d is distance in miles. What does 18 represent?

Answer: D / The fixed charge, in dollars, before any distance charge. C(0) = 18, so 18 is the charge at zero miles. The coefficient 2.5, not 18, is the per-mile rate.

Math · Module 1 · Question 13 of 22

A positive exponential function f satisfies f (0) = 5 and f (2) = 45. Which equation defines f ?

Answer: D / f (x) = 5 · 3x In f (x) = abx, a = 5. Then 5b2 = 45, so b2 = 9. A positive exponential base gives b = 3.

Math · Module 1 · Question 16 of 22

How many distinct real solutions does 2x2 + 4x + 5 = 0 have?

Answer: B / 0 The discriminant is 42 − 4(2)(5) = 16 − 40 = −24. Since it is negative, there are no real roots.

Math · Module 1 · Question 17 of 22

A cone has diameter 8 and perpendicular height 9. Its volume is kπ. What is k?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 48 The radius is 4, so the volume is 13π(42)(9) = 48π. The requested number is the coefficient 48.

Answer: 5 Cross-multiply to obtain 12 = 3(x − 1), so x − 1 = 4 and x = 5. This does not violate the denominator restriction.

Math · Module 1 · Question 22 of 22

For a positive constant p, the equation px2 − 12x + 9 = 0 has exactly one distinct real solution. What is p?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 4 Because p > 0, the equation is genuinely quadratic. A repeated root requires discriminant zero: 144 − 36p = 0. Thus p = 4, giving (2x − 3)2 = 0.

The histogram uses intervals that include their left endpoint and exclude their right endpoint. How many observations are at least 10 but less than 30?

Answer: B / 11 Add the frequencies in the [10, 20) and [20, 30) intervals: 6 + 5 = 11. The first and last bars are outside the requested range.

Math · Module 2 · Question 6 of 22

A linear function satisfies f (2) = 13 and f (7) = 33. What is f (10)?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 45 The slope is (33 − 13)/(7 − 2) = 4. From input 7 to 10, the output increases by 3(4) = 12, so f (10) = 45.

Math · Module 2 · Question 7 of 22

Which ordered pair satisfies both y ≥ 2x − 1 and x + y ≤ 8?

Answer: A / (2, 4) For (2, 4), 4 ≥ 3 and 6 ≤ 8. For (4, 3) the first inequality fails; for (3, 6) the second fails; for (0, −2) the first fails.

Math · Module 2 · Question 8 of 22

A quantity retains 81% of its value every 2 years, following a positive exponential model. What percent of its value does it retain each year?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 90 Let the annual retention factor be b > 0. Then b2 = 0.81, so b = 0.9. This is 90% retention, not 90% loss.

Math · Module 2 · Question 13 of 22

What is the solution to x + 20 = x?

Answer: A / 5 The right side requires x ≥ 0. Squaring gives x2 − x − 20 = (x − 5)(x + 4) = 0. Reject −4, leaving 5; the original equation checks as 25 = 5.

Answer: B / 0 and 6 On the x-axis, y = 0. Thus (x − 3)2 + 16 = 25, so (x − 3)2 = 9 and x = 0 or 6.

Math · Module 2 · Question 18 of 22

The figure is a rectangle of width 12 and height 5 topped by a triangle with the same base and perpendicular height 4. What is the total area?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 84 The rectangle area is 12(5) = 60. The triangle area is 12(12)(4) = 24. Their interiors do not overlap, so the total is 60 + 24 = 84.

Math · Module 2 · Question 19 of 22

For which value of c does x2−4x−2 = c have no real solution?

Answer: A / 4 For x ≠ 2, the left side simplifies to x + 2. The candidate is x = c − 2. It is excluded exactly when c − 2 = 2, so c = 4.

Math · Module 2 · Question 20 of 22

A theater sells adult tickets for $14 and student tickets for $9. It sells 80 tickets for $970. How many adult tickets were sold?

Answer: B / 50 Let a be adult tickets. Then 14a + 9(80 − a) = 970. This gives 5a + 720 = 970, so a = 50. The remaining 30 tickets are student tickets.

Contextual systems / Challenge Review: Example 2.04.

Math · Module 2 · Question 21 of 22

For which value of k does x2 − 6x + k = 0 have two distinct positive real roots?

Answer: D / 8 Two distinct real roots require 36 − 4k > 0, so k < 9. With sum 6 positive, both roots are positive exactly when their product k > 0. Thus 0 < k < 9, and only 8 works.

Root signs and counts / Challenge Review: Example 6.05.

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: −17 Matching coefficients gives 2a = 10, so a = 5. Matching constants gives 3a + b = −7, so b = −22. Therefore a + b = −17.

How this differs from the real SAT

  • Both modules of a section are fixed. On the real exam the second one gets harder or easier depending on how you did on the first.
  • Math includes multiple-choice and numeric-response questions. Enter numeric answers as integers, decimals, or fractions.
  • There is no calculator built in, so use your own. The real exam has a Desmos calculator inside the testing app.
  • You can pause whenever you want, and your answers save in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back. The real exam gives you one ten-minute break and no pause, unless you are approved for breaks as needed.
  • Every question here counts, and you get counts of right, wrong and blank rather than a score on the 400 to 1600 scale.

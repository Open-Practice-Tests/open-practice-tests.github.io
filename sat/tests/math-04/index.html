Math · Module 1 · Question 1 of 22

The table classifies 100 ceramic shards by glaze and edge type. One shard is selected at random from those with a smooth edge. What is the probability that it has a blue glaze?

Answer: D.

The condition restricts the sample space to the smooth-edge column. There are 36+24=6036+24=60 such shards, of which 36 are blue. Thus the probability is 36/60=3/536/60=3/5.

Check: The rough-edge counts are irrelevant once the selected shard is known to have a smooth edge.

A: The smooth-edge sample space has 60 shards, not a denominator producing this value.

B: This uses all 100 shards instead of conditioning on smooth edges.

C: This is the amber proportion within the smooth-edge group.

Watch for: Use the total for the conditioned group, not the grand total.

For what value of k does the system below have no solution?

Answer: C. 4 The left sides become proportional only when k = 4, making the second left side four times the first. But at k = 4, the right side is 18, not 4(7) = 28. Random selection gives a representative starting sample, but selective nonresponse can change its composition. If households with more bags respond more often, the response-only mean can be biased upward.

Check: Increasing the number invited would not automatically remove a systematic difference between responders and nonresponders.

B: Incomplete response can still be informative; the issue is potential bias, not automatic uselessness.

C: Random selection does not prevent selective nonresponse.

D: The nonrespondents’ values were not observed.

Watch for: Random selection and complete, nonselective response are different conditions.

A circle has equation x2 + y2 + Dx + Ey + 45 = 0. Its center is in the first quadrant, and it is tangent to both coordinate axes. What is D + E?

Answer: A. −125 A circle tangent to both axes with first-quadrant center has center (r, r) for radius r > 0. Its equation expands to x2 + y2 − 2rx − 2ry + r2 = 0. Therefore r2 = 45, so r = 35, and D + E = −4r = −125.

Watch for: The first-quadrant condition determines the signs of the center coordinates and hence the linear coefficients.

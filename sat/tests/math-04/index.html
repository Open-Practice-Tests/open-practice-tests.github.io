Math · Module 1 · Question 1 of 22

The function f (t) = m(t − 8) + 17 is linear. If f (14) = 41, what is f (0)?

Answer: B. −15 The condition gives 6m + 17 = 41, so m = 4. Substituting t = 0 gives f (0) = 4(−8) + 17 = −15. The constant 17 is the output at t = 8, not at t = 0.

Watch for: A centered linear form does not display the vertical intercept as its standalone constant.

Math · Module 1 · Question 2 of 22

Which expression is equivalent to 3x2 − 18x + 15?

Answer: B. 3(x − 1)(x − 5) Factor out 3: 3(x2 − 6x + 5). The two numbers with sum −6 and product 5 are −1 and −5, so the expression is 3(x − 1)(x − 5).

Watch for: Check both the linear coefficient and the constant after factoring out the leading coefficient.

Math · Module 1 · Question 3 of 22

The shaded region is the solution set of which system of inequalities? The rising boundary is solid, and the falling boundary is dashed.

Answer: C. y ≥ 32 x − 2 and y < −12 x + 6 The rising line passes through (0, −2) and (4, 4), so its slope is 6/4 = 3/2. Shading is above it, and the solid boundary includes equality. The falling line passes through (0, 6) and (4, 4), so its slope is −2/4 = −1/2. Shading is below it, and the dashed boundary excludes equality. Thus y ≥ 32 x − 2 and y < −12 x + 6.

Watch for: Use both shading direction and boundary type. A correct slope alone does not identify an inequality.

Math · Module 1 · Question 4 of 22

A fitted model is y^ = 4.8 + 0.35x. For one observation, x = 12 and y = 8.4. The model's prediction exceeds the observed value by how much?

Answer: D. 0.6 At x = 12, the prediction is 4.8 + 0.35(12) = 9.0. Subtract the observation: 9.0 − 8.4 = 0.6. The question asks prediction minus observation, the opposite sign from the usual observed-minus-predicted residual.

Watch for: Read the stated order of subtraction instead of automatically reporting the residual.

Math · Module 1 · Question 5 of 22

Real numbers u and v satisfy 2u+v = 128 and 2u−v = 8. What is 4v?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 16 Divide the equations: 2(u+v)−(u−v) = 128/8 = 16. The left side is 22v = 4v, which is exactly the requested quantity. Thus the answer is 16.

Watch for: Subtract the full exponent u − v, so the two v terms add.

Math · Module 1 · Question 6 of 22

Two instruments have linearly related readings p and q. A reading of p = −4 corresponds to q = 17, and a reading of p = 6 corresponds to q = −8. What reading of p corresponds to q = 2?

Answer: C. 2 The rate of change of q with respect to p is (−8 − 17)/(6 − (−4)) = −2.5. Thus q = −2.5p + 7. Setting q = 2 gives −2.5p = −5, so p = 2.

Watch for: Keep track of which reading is the input and which is the output.

Math · Module 1 · Question 7 of 22

A uniform sheet has area 3 square meters, thickness 2 millimeters, and density 1.25 grams per cubic centimeter. What is its mass in kilograms? (1 meter = 100 centimeters; 10 millimeters = 1 centimeter.)

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 7.5 The area is 3(1002) = 30,000 square centimeters, and the thickness is 0.2 centimeter. Thus the volume is 6000 cubic centimeters. Mass is 6000(1.25) = 7500 grams, or 7.5 kilograms.

Watch for: Convert area with a squared factor and thickness with a linear factor before multiplying.

Math · Module 1 · Question 8 of 22

The function f (x) = 5x2 + bx + 20 has minimum value 0, and b > 0. What is b?

Answer: D. 20 A quadratic with positive leading coefficient and minimum 0 has a double real root, so its discriminant is zero: b2 − 4(5)(20) = 0. Hence b2 = 400. The condition b > 0 selects b = 20. Indeed, f (x) = 5(x + 2)2.

Watch for: The sign condition selects the positive coefficient from the two discriminant candidates.

Math · Module 1 · Question 9 of 22

The graph of y = (k − 2)x + 3k + 1 has negative slope and positive y-intercept. Which interval gives all possible values of k?

Answer: C. −1/3 < k < 2 Negative slope means k − 2 < 0, so k < 2. Positive vertical intercept means 3k + 1 > 0, so k > −1/3. Both must hold, giving −1/3 < k < 2.

Watch for: Both sign conditions are strict and must be satisfied simultaneously.

Math · Module 1 · Question 10 of 22

A circular sector has radius 10 and arc length 12π. Its two straight edges are joined without overlap or stretching to form the lateral surface of a right circular cone. What is the height of the cone?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 8 The sector's arc becomes the circumference of the cone's base. Thus 2πr = 12π, so the base radius is 6. The sector radius becomes the slant height, which is 10. A cross-section through the cone's axis gives h2 + 62 = 102, so h2 = 64 and h = 8.

Watch for: The sector radius is the cone's slant height, not its vertical height or base radius.

Math · Module 1 · Question 11 of 22

A quantity is modeled by Q(t) = 680(0.98)6t, where t is measured in years. By what factor is the quantity multiplied over each one-year interval?

Answer: A. (0.98)6 Increasing t by 1 raises the exponent by 6. The output is therefore multiplied by (0.98)6. Equivalently, rewrite the model as 680[(0.98)6]t to display the annual multiplier.

Watch for: The factor 0.98 acts six times per year; it is not the annual factor.

Math · Module 1 · Question 12 of 22

A maker sells each kit for $24. A marketplace keeps 12.5% of the selling price, and materials cost $13 per kit. The maker also pays a fixed $84 event fee. What is the least number of kits that must be sold to earn a profit of at least $100?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 23 The marketplace fee is 0.125(24) = 3 dollars per kit. Each kit therefore contributes 24 − 3 − 13 = 8 dollars toward the fixed fee and profit. Require 8n − 84 ≥ 100, giving n ≥ 23. Selling 23 kits yields exactly $100 profit.

Watch for: The selling price is revenue, not profit; subtract both per-kit expenses before the fixed fee.

Math · Module 1 · Question 13 of 22

Two estimates are reported as intervals. Interval A runs from 63% to 71%, and interval B runs from 62% to 74%. Each interval is centered at its estimate. The margin of error for B is what percent greater than the margin of error for A?

Answer: A. 50% The margins of error are half the interval widths: MA = (71 − 63)/2 = 4 percentage points and MB = (74 − 62)/2 = 6 percentage points. Relative to A, the increase is (6 − 4)/4 = 0.5, or 50%.

Watch for: The margin of error is half the full width; the percent comparison uses A's margin as its base.

Math · Module 1 · Question 14 of 22

The function f (x) = ax − 1 + b, with a ≠ 0, satisfies f (0) = 2 and f (2) = 0. Which value can never be an output of f ?

Answer: B. 1 The conditions give −a + b = 2 and a + b = 0. Adding gives b = 1, and then a = −1. Thus f (x) = 1 − 1/(x − 1). The fraction can never be zero, so the output can never equal 1. Every other real output is possible for an appropriate input.

Watch for: The excluded input is 1 here, but the excluded output must be justified separately from the horizontal shift.

Math · Module 1 · Question 15 of 22

Which equation represents the line through (8, 3) perpendicular to 4x − 7y = 11?

Answer: D. 7x + 4y = 68 The original slope is 4/7, so the perpendicular slope is −7/4. A line with that slope can be written 7x +4y = C. Substituting (8, 3) gives C = 56 + 12 = 68.

Watch for: For perpendicular nonvertical lines, use the negative reciprocal slope, then verify the required point.

Math · Module 1 · Question 16 of 22

In a right triangle, an acute angle has sine 3/5. The triangle's perimeter is 72. What is the hypotenuse length?

Answer: B. 30 The sine ratio makes the opposite leg 3k and hypotenuse 5k. The other leg is 25k2 − 9k2 = 4k. The perimeter is 12k = 72, so k = 6 and the hypotenuse is 5k = 30.

Watch for: A trigonometric ratio gives relative lengths; use the perimeter to determine the scale.

Math · Module 1 · Question 17 of 22

What is the product of all real solutions of (x2 − 5x + 4)2 = 9?

Answer: A. 1 The inner expression must be 3 or −3. The first case gives x2 − 5x + 1 = 0, which has discriminant 21 and two real roots with product 1. The second gives x2 − 5x + 7 = 0, whose discriminant is −3, so it contributes no real roots. The requested product is therefore 1.

Watch for: Both signs of the square root must be considered, but only real solutions contribute to the product.

Math · Module 1 · Question 18 of 22

For every real pair (x, y) satisfying 2x + 5y = 13, the expression 7x + ky has the same value c. What is k + c?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 63 To remain constant as (x, y) varies along the line, the expression must be a multiple of 2x + 5y. Matching the x coefficient gives multiplier 7/2. Hence k = (7/2)(5) = 35/2 and c = (7/2)(13) = 91/2. Their sum is 126/2 = 63. One can also substitute x = (13 − 5y)/2 and require the y coefficient to vanish.

Watch for: The condition applies to every solution pair, not just one conveniently chosen pair.

Math · Module 1 · Question 19 of 22

Data set A is 8, 8, 12, 12. For a positive number p, data set B is 8 − p, 8 + p, 12 − p, 12 + p. Which statement must be true?

Answer: D. The means are equal, and the population standard deviation of B is greater. The opposite shifts cancel in the sum, so both means are 10. The squared deviations for A sum to 16. For B, pairing the opposite shifts gives (−2 − p)2 + (−2 + p)2 + (2 − p)2 + (2 + p)2 = 16 + 4p2. Since p > 0, this is larger, while the count stays 4. Thus B has greater population standard deviation.

Watch for: Some observations can move toward the mean even though the total squared deviation increases.

Math · Module 1 · Question 20 of 22

A right circular cone has height 15 and base radius 10. A plane parallel to the base cuts off a smaller cone whose height, measured from the original vertex, is 6. What is the volume of the remaining solid?

Answer: C. 468π The original volume is 13π(10)2(15) = 500π. The removed cone has length scale 6/15 = 2/5, so its volume is (2/5)3(500π) = 32π. The remaining volume is 500π − 32π = 468π.

Watch for: The stated 6 is the removed cone's height, not the remaining solid's height.

Math · Module 1 · Question 21 of 22

The inequality kx + 6 ≤ 2x + 15 must hold for every real x in the interval −2 ≤ x ≤ 5. What is the greatest possible value of k?

Answer: A. 19/5 Rewrite as (k − 2)x ≤ 9. At x = 5, a necessary condition is 5(k − 2) ≤ 9, so k ≤ 19/5. This bound works: for k = 19/5, the left side is (9/5)x, whose largest value on the interval occurs at x = 5 and equals 9. Hence the greatest possible k is 19/5.

Watch for: A condition that must hold for every input is controlled by the worst input, not by a single arbitrary test value.

Math · Module 1 · Question 22 of 22

Positive real numbers a and b satisfy a + b = 9 and a + b = 45. What is ab?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 324 Square the first equation: a + b + 2ab = 81. Using a + b = 45 gives 2ab = 36, so ab = 18 and ab = 324. The positive pair a = 36, b = 9 verifies consistency.

Watch for: After finding ab, square once more to report the product itself.

Math · Module 2 · Question 1 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (−2) = 5 and f (4) = −7. For what value of x is f (x) = x?

Answer: C. 1/3 The slope is (−7 − 5)/(4 − (−2)) = −2, and the intercept is 1, so f (x) = −2x + 1. Solve −2x + 1 = x to obtain 3x = 1, or x = 1/3.

Watch for: Equating an output to its input is not the same as finding a zero.

Math · Module 2 · Question 2 of 22

Let f (x) = x − 5 and g(x) = f (3x + 2). What is the domain of g over the real numbers?

Answer: C. x ≥ 1 Substitute into the entire rule: g(x) = 3x + 2 − 5 = 3x − 3. The radicand must be nonnegative, so 3x − 3 ≥ 0, giving x ≥ 1. Equality is allowed because the square root of zero is defined.

Watch for: Apply the original domain condition to the transformed input, not to the new variable alone.

Math · Module 2 · Question 3 of 22

A store raises an item's price by 10%. The number sold decreases, but the revenue from the item increases by 4.5%. By what percent did the number sold decrease?

Answer: B. 5% Let r be the fraction of the original quantity still sold. Revenue is price times quantity, so 1.10r = 1.045. Hence r = 0.95, meaning the quantity decreased by 1 − 0.95 = 0.05, or 5%.

Watch for: Changes in price and quantity multiply; their percentage changes do not simply subtract.

Math · Module 2 · Question 4 of 22

What is the greatest integer x satisfying 0.3(5x − 2) < 0.8(x + 8)?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 9 Expand: 1.5x − 0.6 < 0.8x + 6.4. Thus 0.7x < 7, giving x < 10. Because the inequality is strict, the greatest allowed integer is 9.

Watch for: An exact equality at 10 does not satisfy a strict inequality.

Math · Module 2 · Question 5 of 22

The equation |3x − 8| + 2 = 14 has solutions r and s, where r < s. What is s − r?

Answer: D. 8 Isolate the absolute value: |3x − 8| = 12. Then 3x − 8 = −12 or 12, giving r = −4/3 and s = 20/3. Their difference is 24/3 = 8.

Watch for: The separation in the inner expression is 24, but the coefficient 3 scales the input separation.

Math · Module 2 · Question 6 of 22

Two concentric circles have outer circumference three times the inner circumference. The area between them is 128π. What is the difference between their radii?

Answer: D. 8 Circumference is proportional to radius, so if the inner radius is r, the outer radius is 3r. The annulus area is π(9r2 − r2) = 8πr2 = 128π. Hence r2 = 16, r = 4, and the radius difference is 12 − 4 = 8.

Watch for: A circumference ratio gives a length ratio; the area difference uses squared radii.

Math · Module 2 · Question 7 of 22

A line through (k, 2k − 1) and (k + 4, 3k + 7) has slope 5/2. What is k?

Answer: C. 2 The horizontal change is 4, and the vertical change is (3k + 7) − (2k − 1) = k + 8. Thus (k + 8)/4 = 5/2, giving k + 8 = 10 and k = 2. The points are always distinct because their x-coordinates differ by 4.

Watch for: The subtraction of 2k − 1 adds 1 to the vertical difference.

Math · Module 2 · Question 8 of 22

Let f (x) = x2 − 6x + 10. What is the minimum value of f (x) + f (6 − x) over all real x?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 2 Complete the square: f (x) = (x − 3)2 + 1. Then f (6 − x) = (3 − x)2 + 1 = (x − 3)2 + 1. Their sum is 2(x − 3)2 + 2, whose minimum is 2 at x = 3.

Watch for: The two inputs are reflections about the symmetry axis and therefore produce equal outputs.

Math · Module 2 · Question 9 of 22

Researchers randomly sample members from a robotics league's complete membership list. Members who report more simulator practice tend to have higher competition scores. Which conclusion is best supported by the design?

Answer: C. The sample provides evidence of an association among league members, but does not establish that simulator practice causes higher scores. Random sampling supports extending an observed association to the sampled population, subject to sampling uncertainty. But practice levels were observed rather than randomly assigned, so experience, motivation, or other factors may explain some of the association. The design does not isolate a causal effect.

Watch for: Random sampling and random assignment answer different questions: representation versus causal comparison.

Math · Module 2 · Question 10 of 22

For what real value of a does the system (a + 1)x + 2y = 7 and 3x + 6y = 21 have no solution?

Answer: B. There is no such real value of a. Divide the second equation by 3 to get x + 2y = 7. When a = 0, the equations are identical, so there are infinitely many solutions. When a ≠ 0, subtracting yields ax = 0, so x = 0 and y = 7/2, a unique solution. No value of a gives a contradiction.

Watch for: The only parallel case is also coincident; do not assume a zero determinant means no solution.

Math · Module 2 · Question 11 of 22

For x ≥ 0, what value of x satisfies x1/2 + x1/4 = 6?

Answer: B. 16 Let u = x1/4, so u ≥ 0 and x1/2 = u2. Then u2 + u − 6 = (u + 3)(u − 2) = 0. Only u = 2 is nonnegative. Therefore x = u4 = 16.

Watch for: The substituted variable is a nonnegative fourth root, so the quadratic root u = −3 is inadmissible.

Math · Module 2 · Question 12 of 22

A rectangular box with interior dimensions 18 by 12 by 6 contains four nonoverlapping spheres of radius 3, each wholly inside the box. The volume inside the box but outside the spheres is 1296 − kπ. What is k?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 144 The box volume is 18(12)(6) = 1296. Each sphere has volume 43π(3)3 = 36π, so four spheres occupy 144π. The unoccupied volume is 1296 − 144π, giving k = 144.

Watch for: Subtract the volume of all four spheres, not just one.

Math · Module 2 · Question 13 of 22

Technician A completes 12 more calibrations per hour than technician B. Together, 5 hours of work by A and 8 hours of work by B produce 255 calibrations. What is the ratio of A's hourly rate to B's hourly rate?

Answer: A. 9/5 Let B's rate be r, so A's rate is r +12. The combined-work condition is 5(r +12)+8r = 255. Thus 13r +60 = 255, giving r = 15. The rates are 27 and 15, so their ratio is 27/15 = 9/5.

Watch for: Work totals are rate times time, and the final target is the ratio of rates rather than either rate.

Math · Module 2 · Question 14 of 22

For how many integer values of b does (x − 2)(x2 + bx + 12) have exactly one distinct real zero?

Answer: D. 13 The linear factor always gives the zero 2. The quadratic must have no real roots: if it had two distinct real roots, at least one would be different from 2; if it had a double root, that root could not be 2 because its product would be 4 rather than 12. Thus b2 − 48 < 0. The integer values are −6 through 6, a total of 13.

Watch for: Count distinct real zeros of the entire product, and check whether a quadratic root could coincide with 2.

Math · Module 2 · Question 15 of 22

A bag contains 6 gold counters and some silver counters. Two counters are chosen at random without replacement. The probability that both are gold is 1/7. How many silver counters are in the bag?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 9 Let the total number be n. Then (6/n)(5/(n − 1)) = 1/7, giving n(n − 1) = 210. Thus n2 − n − 210 = (n − 15)(n + 14) = 0, and the positive count is n = 15. There are 15 − 6 = 9 silver counters.

Watch for: The total count falls by 1 after the first draw; report the silver count, not the total count.

Math · Module 2 · Question 16 of 22

A signal's strength is modeled by S(t) = 2300(0.87)t/2, where t ≥ 0 is measured in seconds. What is the first whole-number second at which the model predicts a strength less than 900?

Answer: A. 14 The model is strictly decreasing. A calculator gives S(13) ≈ 930.3 and S(14) ≈ 867.7. Thus 13 seconds is too early, while 14 seconds meets the strict bound. Since the outputs decrease with time, 14 is the first whole-number second that works.

Watch for: A decimal threshold is not the requested whole-number time; check adjacent integer inputs.

Math · Module 2 · Question 17 of 22

The function f (x) = (a − 2)x + a + 4 has integer parameter a and satisfies f (1) > 9 and f (3) < 17. What is the x-coordinate of its x-intercept?

Answer: A. −4 The first inequality gives 2a + 2 > 9, or a > 3.5. The second gives 4a − 2 < 17, or a < 4.75. The only integer is a = 4. Then f (x) = 2x + 8, whose zero is x = −4.

Watch for: Find the allowed integer parameter before using the line's intercept formula.

Math · Module 2 · Question 18 of 22

Two similar triangles have areas 96 and 216. Their corresponding heights differ by 6. What is the greater of those two heights?

Answer: D. 18 The smaller-to-larger area ratio is 96/216 = 4/9, so the height ratio is 2/3. Write the heights as 2u and 3u. Their difference is u = 6, giving the greater height 3u = 18.

Watch for: Convert the area ratio to a length ratio before applying the given difference.

Math · Module 2 · Question 19 of 22

The graphs of f (x) = x2 + ax + 12 and g(x) = 2x2 − 4x + b intersect at inputs x = 1 and x = 5. What is a + b?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 19 The difference g(x) − f (x) = x2 − (a + 4)x + (b − 12) has roots 1 and 5 and leading coefficient 1. It must equal (x − 1)(x − 5) = x2 − 6x + 5. Therefore a + 4 = 6 and b − 12 = 5, giving a = 2, b = 17, and sum 19.

Watch for: An intersection makes the difference of the functions zero, not either individual function zero.

Math · Module 2 · Question 20 of 22

A rectangle has area 84 and diagonal length 193. What is its perimeter?

Answer: A. 38 Let the side lengths be l, w > 0. The area gives lw = 84, and the diagonal gives l2 + w2 = 193. Then (l + w)2 = 193 + 2(84) = 361, so l + w = 19. The perimeter is 2(19) = 38. One consistent rectangle has sides 7 and 12.

Watch for: The diagonal gives the sum of the squares of the sides, not the square of their sum.

Math · Module 2 · Question 21 of 22

For a linear function f and a fixed real number t, f (t + 2) = 3 f (t) and f (t + 8) = f (t) + 48. What is f (t + 5)?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 36 The second condition gives slope 48/8 = 6. Therefore f (t + 2) = f (t) + 12. Compare this with 3 f (t) to get f (t) = 6. The output five input units later is f (t + 5) = 6 + 5(6) = 36.

Watch for: The value of t itself is unnecessary; linear output changes depend only on input differences.

Math · Module 2 · Question 22 of 22

A quadratic function f with leading coefficient 1 satisfies f (1) = f (7) and has minimum value −4. What is the sum of the squares of all real solutions of | f (x)| = 3?

Answer: B. 80 Equal outputs put the axis at x = 4, so f (x) = (x − 4)2 − 4. The equation | f (x)| = 3 gives (x − 4)2 = 7 or (x − 4)2 = 1. For a pair 4 ± d, the squared values sum to 2(16 + d2). Thus the total is 2(16 + 7) + 2(16 + 1) = 46 + 34 = 80. All four roots are real and distinct.

Watch for: Include both f (x) = 3 and f (x) = −3 before computing the requested sum.

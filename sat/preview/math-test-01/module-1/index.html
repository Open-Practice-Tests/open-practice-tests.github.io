Math · Module 1 · Question 1 of 22

A print shop charges a fixed setup fee plus a constant price per poster. An order of 8 posters costs $91, and adding 5 posters to any order increases the charge by $32.50. What is the charge for 12 posters?

Answer: D. $117 The price per poster is 32.50/5 = 6.50 dollars. Moving from 8 to 12 posters adds 4 posters, so the charge is 91 + 4(6.50) = 117 dollars. The setup fee is already included in the $91; it is not charged again.

Watch for: Do not add the cost of five posters when the order increases by only four.

Math · Module 1 · Question 2 of 22

For every real value of x, which number is equal to 9x+132x−1?

Answer: D. 27 Write 9 as 32. The quotient is 32x+2/32x−1 = 3(2x+2)−(2x−1) = 33 = 27. The variable terms cancel, so the expression is constant.

Watch for: Subtract the entire denominator exponent, including its negative constant.

Math · Module 1 · Question 3 of 22

An archive removes 15% of its files during a first review. It then removes 20% of the files that remain. What percent of the original files remain after both reviews?

Answer: C. 68% Let the original count be 100. After the first review, 85 remain. The second review keeps 80% of those, or 0.80(85) = 68. Thus 68% of the original files remain.

Watch for: The second percentage uses the remaining files, not the original count.

Math · Module 1 · Question 4 of 22

In triangle ABC, side BC is extended through C to point D. Ray CE bisects exterior angle ACD, and angle ACE measures 67°. Angle A is 22° greater than angle B. What is the measure of angle B, in degrees?

Answer: B. 56 Because CE bisects the exterior angle, angle ACD measures 2(67) = 134°. The exterior-angle theorem gives A + B = 134°. With A = B + 22°, this becomes 2B + 22 = 134, so B = 56°. The other angles are A = 78° and C = 46°, which sum with B to 180°.

Watch for: Double the bisected exterior angle before applying the exterior-angle theorem.

Math · Module 1 · Question 5 of 22

Real numbers u and v satisfy 4u + v = 29 and u − 3v = 7. What is the value of 10u − 4v?

Answer: A. 72 Add the equations to obtain 5u − 2v = 36. The requested expression is twice that left side, so 10u − 4v = 2(36) = 72. Solving separately for u and v is unnecessary.

Watch for: Match both coefficients in the requested expression before choosing an operation.

Math · Module 1 · Question 6 of 22

The equation (x − 7)2 − 20 = 5 has two real solutions. What is the positive difference between them?

Answer: C. 10 Isolate the square: (x − 7)2 = 25. Thus x − 7 = 5 or x − 7 = −5, giving x = 12 and x = 2. Their positive difference is 12 − 2 = 10.

Watch for: The square root gives each solution's distance from 7, not the distance between the solutions.

Math · Module 1 · Question 8 of 22

The lines 6x − 5y = 20 and kx + 10y = 7 are parallel. What is the value of k?

Answer: B. −12 The first line has slope 6/5, while the second has slope −k/10. Parallel lines have equal slopes, so −k/10 = 6/5, giving k = −12. Their intercepts differ, so the lines are distinct.

Watch for: In Ax + By = C, the slope is −A/B, not A/B.

Math · Module 1 · Question 9 of 22

What is the sum of all real solutions of |2x − 9| = x + 3?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 14 For 2x − 9 ≥ 0, solve 2x − 9 = x + 3, obtaining x = 12. For 2x − 9 < 0, solve 9 − 2x = x + 3, obtaining x = 2. Both satisfy their branch conditions and the original equation. Their sum is 14.

Watch for: Check branch conditions and the original equation; the right side of an absolute-value equation must be nonnegative.

Math · Module 1 · Question 10 of 22

A workshop pays $54 to reserve equipment and $7.50 for each kit it uses. It can spend at most $208 in total. What is the greatest number of whole kits it can use?

Answer: B. 20 If n is the number of kits, then 54 + 7.50n ≤ 208. Thus n ≤ 154/7.50 = 20.533 . . .. The largest allowed integer is 20. Twenty kits cost $204, whereas 21 cost $211.50.

Watch for: Round downward because the quantity must be a whole number that does not exceed the budget.

Math · Module 1 · Question 11 of 22

Two parallel chords of a circle lie on opposite sides of its center. Their lengths are 16 and 30, and the perpendicular distance between the chords is 23. What is the radius of the circle?

Answer: C. 17 Let the distances from the center to the shorter and longer chords be a and b. Then a +b = 23. Perpendiculars from the center bisect the chords, so r2 = a2 + 82 = b2 + 152. Hence a2 − b2 = 161, and (a − b)(a + b) = 161 gives a − b = 7. Thus a = 15, b = 8, and r2 = 152 + 82 = 289, so r = 17.

Watch for: The chord lengths must first be halved. Opposite sides of the center make the two perpendicular distances add to 23.

Math · Module 1 · Question 12 of 22

The value of an instrument is modeled by V(t) = 1800(0.81)t/6, where t is measured in years. According to the model, by what percent does the value decrease over each 3-year interval?

Answer: A. 10% Increasing t by 3 multiplies the output by (0.81)3/6 = 0.81 = 0.9. A remaining-value factor of 0.9 corresponds to a decrease of 1 − 0.9 = 0.10, or 10%.

Watch for: The stated 19% decrease occurs over six years, not three.

Math · Module 1 · Question 13 of 22

A linear function f satisfies f (2) = 9 and f (8) = 33. If f (k) = 2 f (k − 3), what is k?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 23/4 The slope is (33−9)/(8−2) = 4, so f (x) = 4x +1. Substituting into the condition gives 4k +1 = 2[4(k −3)+1] = 8k − 22. Therefore 4k = 23, and k = 23/4.

Watch for: f (k − 3) subtracts 3 from the input; it is not f (k) − 3.

Math · Module 1 · Question 14 of 22

A college randomly selects 200 entries from a complete list of visits to its library during weekday mornings in one month. For 62% of those visits, the visitor used a study room. Which population is most directly represented by this sampling method?

Answer: A. All weekday-morning visits to the library during that month The sample was selected from a list of visits, not a list of distinct people, and the list covers only weekday mornings in one month. The directly represented population is therefore all visits within that same time frame. A person who visited repeatedly may contribute several entries.

Watch for: The unit sampled is a visit, not a unique student, and the sampling frame limits the time period.

Math · Module 1 · Question 15 of 22

For what value of k does the system below have no solution?

x + 2y = 7

kx + 8y = k + 14

Answer: C. 4 The left sides become proportional only when k = 4, making the second left side four times the first. But at k = 4, the right side is 18, not 4(7) = 28. Thus the lines are distinct and parallel, with no solution. For other k, eliminating y leaves a nonzero coefficient of x, so there is one solution.

Watch for: Proportional coefficients do not guarantee infinitely many solutions; compare the constants too.

Math · Module 1 · Question 16 of 22

The polynomial P(x) = x3 + cx2 − 7x − 10 has x − 2 as a factor. What is P(1)?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: −12 The factor condition gives P(2) = 0, so 8 + 4c − 14 − 10 = 0. Thus c = 4. Now P(1) = 1 + 4 − 7 − 10 = −12.

Watch for: A factor x − 2 implies evaluation at positive 2, not negative 2.

Math · Module 1 · Question 18 of 22

For what value of a does (a − 4)(x + 2) = 3x + 2a + 9 have no real solution for x?

Answer: D. 7 Expand and collect terms: (a − 4)x + 2a − 8 = 3x + 2a + 9, so (a − 7)x = 17. If a = 7, this becomes 0 = 17, which is impossible. For every other a, the equation has the unique solution x = 17/(a − 7).

Watch for: A zero coefficient can create a contradiction rather than a solution of zero.

Math · Module 1 · Question 19 of 22

In triangle ABC, point D lies on AB and point E lies on AC, with DE ∥ BC. If AD : DB = 3 : 2 and the area of triangle ADE is 54, what is the area of quadrilateral DBCE?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 96 The full side ratio is AD/AB = 3/(3 + 2) = 3/5. Thus the small-to-large area ratio is 9/25. The area of triangle ABC is 54(25/9) = 150. Removing triangle ADE leaves 150 − 54 = 96.

Watch for: The ratio 3 : 2 compares two side pieces, not the small triangle to the full triangle.

Math · Module 1 · Question 20 of 22

Which value of x satisfies x + 7 + 2 = 3x + 9?

Answer: A. 9 Squaring gives x + 11 + 4x + 7 = 3x + 9, so x − 1 = 2x + 7 and therefore x ≥ 1. Squaring again gives (x − 1)2 = 4x + 28, or (x − 9)(x + 3) = 0. Only x = 9 meets x ≥ 1. It also checks: 16 + 2 = 36.

Watch for: Two squarings may produce extraneous candidates; x = −3 fails the original equation.

Math · Module 1 · Question 21 of 22

A laser engraver charges a fixed design-preparation fee plus a constant price per token. Its regular charges are $20 for 80 tokens and $29 for 140 tokens. A priority order adds 20% to the fixed fee but does not change the per-token price. What is the priority charge for 200 tokens?

Answer: B. $39.60 The per-token price is (29 − 20)/(140 − 80) = 0.15 dollar. The regular fixed fee is 20 − 80(0.15) = 8 dollars. The priority fixed fee is 1.20(8) = 9.60 dollars. Therefore the priority charge is 9.60 + 200(0.15) = 39.60 dollars.

Watch for: The 20% surcharge applies only to the fixed fee, not to the entire regular charge.

Math · Module 1 · Question 22 of 22

The equation x2 − 18x + c = 0 has two positive roots. The larger root is three times the smaller root. What is c?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 243/4 Let the smaller root be r and the larger root be 3r. Their sum is 18, so 4r = 18 and r = 9/2. The product of the roots equals c, giving c = (9/2)(27/2) = 243/4.

Watch for: The coefficient of x gives the negative of the root sum, while the constant gives the product.

Math · Module 2 · Question 1 of 22

The graph shows the linear function f . If f (3a) − f (a) = 12, what is a?

Answer: C. −3 The graph passes through (0, 8) and (4, 0), so f (x) = −2x +8. Therefore f (3a)− f (a) = (−6a +8)−(−2a +8) = −4a. Setting this equal to 12 gives a = −3.

Watch for: The vertical intercept cancels when two outputs of the same linear function are subtracted.

Math · Module 2 · Question 2 of 22

The only zeros of a function f are −5 and 7. The function g is defined by g(x) = f (3x + 2). What is the sum of the zeros of g?

Answer: B. −2/3 For g(x) to equal zero, 3x + 2 must equal −5 or 7. The zeros are therefore −7/3 and 5/3, whose sum is −2/3.

Watch for: Transform the inputs by solving 3x + 2 = r; do not apply 3r + 2 to the old zeros.

Math · Module 2 · Question 3 of 22

A solid has density 2.4 grams per cubic centimeter and volume 0.000125 cubic meter. What is its mass, in grams? (1 meter = 100 centimeters.)

Answer: D. 300 One cubic meter contains 1003 = 1,000,000 cubic centimeters. Thus the solid has volume 0.000125(1,000,000) = 125 cubic centimeters. Its mass is density times volume: 2.4(125) = 300 grams.

Watch for: Cube the length conversion factor when converting cubic units.

Math · Module 2 · Question 4 of 22

Angle θ is acute and tan θ = 4/7. Which expression equals sin θ?

Answer: D. 465

Use a right triangle with opposite leg 4 and adjacent leg 7. Its hypotenuse is 42 + 72 = 65. Since sine is opposite divided by hypotenuse, sin θ = 4/65. Any scaled triangle gives the same ratio.

Watch for: The denominator of tangent is the adjacent leg; the denominator of sine is the hypotenuse.

Math · Module 2 · Question 5 of 22

Real numbers x and y satisfy y ≥ 12x + 3 and y < −x + 12. What is the greatest integer that x can equal?

Answer: C. 5 A permissible y exists only when the lower bound is strictly below the upper bound: 12 x + 3 < −x + 12. Thus 32 x < 9, or x < 6. The largest integer is 5; for example, x = 5 allows 5.5 ≤ y < 7.

Watch for: Equality at x = 6 is impossible because the second inequality is strict.

Math · Module 2 · Question 6 of 22

Positive numbers a and b satisfy a2b = 54 and ab2 = 24. What is a/b?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 9/4 Divide the equations. Since a, b > 0, division is valid: (a2b)/(ab2) = 54/24. The left side simplifies to a/b, and the right side reduces to 9/4.

Watch for: Solving for both positive numbers is unnecessary when their ratio is directly available.

Math · Module 2 · Question 7 of 22

A theater sold 70 tickets. Premium tickets cost $18 each, and regular tickets cost $11 each. The total revenue was at least $980. What is the least possible number of premium tickets?

Answer: A. 30 Let p be the premium-ticket count. Then the regular-ticket count is 70 − p, so 18p + 11(70 − p) ≥ 980. Simplifying gives 7p + 770 ≥ 980, or p ≥ 30. Exactly 30 premium and 40 regular tickets produce $980.

Watch for: Each premium ticket replaces a regular ticket, so its extra contribution is $7, not $18.

Math · Module 2 · Question 8 of 22

The graph of a quadratic function f has vertex (3, −2) and passes through (1, 10). If g(x) = f (x +2)− f (x), what is g(4)?

Answer: A. 24 Write f (x) = a(x − 3)2 − 2. Using (1, 10) gives 10 = 4a − 2, so a = 3. Then g(4) = f (6) − f (4) = [3(3)2 − 2] − [3(1)2 − 2] = 25 − 1 = 24.

Watch for: An input shift occurs before evaluation; g(4) is f (6) − f (4).

Math · Module 2 · Question 9 of 22

A cylinder of radius r and height 5 has the same volume as a right circular cone of radius 2r, where r > 0. What is the height of the cone?

Answer: C. 15/4 The cylinder volume is 5πr2. If the cone height is h, its volume is 13π(2r)2 h = 43πr2 h. Set the volumes equal and divide by πr2 > 0: 5 = 4h/3. Hence h = 15/4.

Watch for: Doubling the radius multiplies the base area by 4, and the cone formula includes a factor of 1/3.

Math · Module 2 · Question 10 of 22

Data set A has mean 18, median 16, and standard deviation 4. Data set B is formed by replacing every value x in A with kx + b, where k > 0. Set B has mean 7 and standard deviation 10. What is its median?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 2 Standard deviation is multiplied by the positive factor k, so 4k = 10 and k = 2.5. The mean gives 2.5(18) + b = 7, so b = −38. Because the transformation is increasing, the median transforms in the same way: 2.5(16) − 38 = 2.

Watch for: The shift b changes the center but not the standard deviation.

Math · Module 2 · Question 11 of 22

Nonnegative real numbers x and y satisfy 4x + 3y = 48 and y ≥ x + 2. What is the greatest possible value of x?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 6 Substitute the lower bound for y into the total: 48 = 4x + 3y ≥ 4x + 3(x + 2) = 7x + 6. Hence x ≤ 6. Equality is attainable at (x, y) = (6, 8), so the maximum is 6.

Watch for: An upper bound must also be attainable before it can be called the maximum.

Math · Module 2 · Question 12 of 22

For all real x, (2x + a)(x − 3) + b(x + 1) = 2x2 + 5x − 7, where a and b are constants. What is a + b?

Answer: A. 11 Expand the left side: 2x2 + (a + b − 6)x + (−3a + b). Matching the coefficient of x gives a + b − 6 = 5, so a + b = 11 immediately. The constant equation is consistent: a = 4.5 and b = 6.5.

Watch for: Match the coefficient that contains the requested combination rather than solving unnecessary separate values.

Math · Module 2 · Question 13 of 22

In a right triangle, the altitude from the right-angle vertex divides the hypotenuse into segments of lengths 8 and 18. What is the area of the triangle?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 156 The two smaller right triangles are similar, which yields h/8 = 18/h and therefore h2 = 144. The altitude is 12, and the full hypotenuse is 8 + 18 = 26. Using that side as the base, the area is 12(26)(12) = 156.

Watch for: The altitude is not either of the original triangle's legs. Derive its length from similarity.

Math · Module 2 · Question 14 of 22

An exponential function R has the form R(t) = abt, where a > 0 and b > 0. For all real t, R(t + 4)/R(t) = 81/16. If R(1) = 12, what is R(3)?

Answer: B. 27 The ratio condition gives b4 = 81/16. Since b > 0, b2 = 9/4. The input increases from 1 to 3 by 2, so R(3) = R(1)b2 = 12(9/4) = 27.

Watch for: The factor 81/16 acts over four input units, not two.

Math · Module 2 · Question 15 of 22

The linear function g(x) = mx + b has m < 0 and satisfies g(g(x)) = 9x + 8 for every real x. What is g(5)?

Answer: A. −19 Composition gives g(g(x)) = m2x + b(m + 1). Matching coefficients yields m2 = 9 and b(m + 1) = 8. Because m < 0, m = −3, so −2b = 8 and b = −4. Then g(5) = −3(5) − 4 = −19.

Watch for: The negative-slope condition selects m = −3 rather than m = 3.

Math · Module 2 · Question 16 of 22

A random sample is used to estimate that 38% of a park's visitors arrive by bicycle, with a margin of error of 6 percentage points. Which interval corresponds to the proportion of visitors who do not arrive by bicycle?

Answer: B. 56% to 68% The bicycle-use interval is from 38 − 6 = 32% to 38 + 6 = 44%. For the complementary proportion, subtract each endpoint from 100% and reverse their order: 100 − 44 = 56% and 100 − 32 = 68%. Thus the corresponding interval is 56% to 68%. Its center is 62%, and the margin is still 6 percentage points; it is not a guarantee about the population.

Watch for: Complementing an interval reverses which endpoint is the lower bound.

Math · Module 2 · Question 17 of 22

For exactly two real values of k, the equation kx − 6x2 − 9 = 1 has exactly one real solution. What is the sum of the squares of those two values of k?

Answer: B. 8 The domain excludes x = 3 and x = −3. Clearing the denominator gives x2 − kx − 3 = 0, whose discriminant k2 + 12 is always positive, so there are always two distinct real candidates. Exactly one is excluded when x = 3 is a root, giving k = 2, or when x = −3 is a root, giving k = −2. Both cannot be roots because their product would be −9, not −3. The target is 22 + (−2)2 = 8.

Watch for: The quadratic discriminant counts candidates, not valid solutions of the rational equation.

Math · Module 2 · Question 18 of 22

The line y = 2x + b is tangent to the circle (x − 2)2 + (y + 1)2 = 20. There are two possible values of b. What is their sum?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: −10 Substitute y = 2x + b into the circle: (x − 2)2 + (2x + b + 1)2 = 20. This becomes 5x2 + 4bx + b2 + 2b − 15 = 0. Tangency requires discriminant zero: 16b2 − 20(b2 + 2b − 15) = 0, or b2 + 10b − 75 = 0. The two values are 5 and −15, whose sum is −10.

Watch for: There are two parallel tangent lines; retain both possible intercepts.

Math · Module 2 · Question 19 of 22

Linear functions f and g satisfy f (0) = g(0), f (4) = 22, and g(6) = 22. If f (1) − g(1) = 2, what is f (0)?

Answer: D. −2 Let the common intercept be b. The slopes are (22 − b)/4 and (22 − b)/6. Their difference is f (1) − g(1) = 2, because the intercepts cancel. Thus (22 − b)(1/4 − 1/6) = 2, giving (22 − b)/12 = 2 and b = −2.

Watch for: The functions share an intercept, not a slope.

Math · Module 2 · Question 20 of 22

For how many integer values of k does (x − 1)(x − 7) = k have exactly one real solution in the interval 0 ≤ x ≤ 6?

Answer: C. 13 Rewrite the equation as (x − 4)2 = k + 9. At k = −9, the only root is x = 4, which is allowed. For k > −9, the roots are 4 ± k + 9. Both lie in [0, 6] when 0 < k + 9 ≤ 2. Exactly the smaller root lies in the interval when 2 < k + 9 ≤ 4, or −5 < k ≤ 7. These integers are −4 through 7, giving 12 values, plus the vertex case k = −9, for a total of 13. All other k give zero or two allowed roots.

Watch for: A double root at the vertex counts once. At k = −5, both x = 2 and x = 6 are allowed, so that endpoint must be excluded.

Math · Module 2 · Question 21 of 22

A shipment contains only 9-kilogram and 14-kilogram containers, with total mass 235 kilograms. What is the greatest possible number of 9-kilogram containers?

Enter an integer, decimal, or fraction. Up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus. No units or mixed numbers.

Answer: 23 Let x and y count the 9- and 14-kilogram containers. Then 9x + 14y = 235, with x, y nonnegative integers. To maximize x, minimize y. Testing y = 0, 1, 2 gives remainders 235, 221, 207 for the 9-kilogram containers. The first divisible by 9 is 207 = 9(23), so y = 2 and x = 23. Larger y only decreases x.

Watch for: The continuous bound 235/9 is not attainable with whole containers of the given sizes.

Math · Module 2 · Question 22 of 22

For how many integer values of c does x2 − 14x + c = 0 have two distinct real roots, both greater than 5?

Answer: D. 3 Complete the square: (x − 7)2 = 49 − c. Two distinct real roots require c < 49. The smaller root is 7 − 49 − c, which must exceed 5. Thus 49 − c < 2, giving c > 45. The integers are 46, 47, 48, so there are 3.

Watch for: Both endpoints are excluded: c = 45 gives a root equal to 5, and c = 49 gives a double root.

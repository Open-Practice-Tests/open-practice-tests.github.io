Question 1

  • Guide question E3A

A data set has value 1 occurring twice, value 2 occurring three times, and value 4 occurring five times. What is its mean?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 2.8

The weighted sum is 1(2) + 2(3) + 4(5) = 28 across 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 observations. The mean is 28/10 = 2.8. Averaging the three distinct labels would ignore their frequencies.

Question 2

  • Guide question E3B

A data set has mean 10 and standard deviation 4. Subtract 6 from every value. What are the new mean and standard deviation?

Why this answer

Answer: D: mean 4; standard deviation 4

The mean shifts down by 6, from 10 to 4. Every value and the mean shift together, so deviations from the mean are unchanged; the standard deviation stays 4. A standard deviation cannot be negative.

Question 3

  • Guide question E3C

Seven numbers have mean 14. An eighth number is added, raising the mean to 15. What is the added number?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 22

The old total is 7(14) = 98; the new total is 8(15) = 120. The added number is 120 − 98 = 22. It must be above the original mean in order to raise it.

Question 4

  • Guide question E3D

What is the median of 2, 5, 9, 12, 18, 21?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 10.5

The six values are already ordered. Average positions 3 and 4: (9 + 12)/2 = 10.5. A median need not itself be an observation when the data count is even.

Question 5

  • Guide question E3E

Data set A is 1, 4, 4, 4, 7 and B is 1, 1, 4, 7, 7. Which has the greater standard deviation?

Why this answer

Answer: B

Both means are 4. A has two observations 3 units from the mean; B has four. Their squared-deviation sums are 18 and 36, respectively. With equal data counts and a consistent convention, B has greater standard deviation.

Review the explanations

Each explanation identifies a valid method and the requested result. Use the linked review target when the difficulty repeats. An alternative method is valid if it preserves all conditions and reaches the same answer.

A productive explanation review Compare the first step in your work with the first step justified by the problem. Identify the earliest mismatch. Rebuild the solution with the worked solution hidden. Then test the repaired behavior on a fresh item. Use the error-analysis record when the pattern is recurring.

Read the metadata correctly

Skill tags are shown here, not on mixed or rehearsal question pages. Foundation, Standard, and Challenge are editorial labels. They do not assign official item difficulty, scale scores, or claim that questions in separate sets are statistically comparable.

Numeric answers

The key gives one correct value. Equivalent fractions or exact terminating decimals represent the same value unless a prompt imposes another form. For an actual digital test, follow the response-entry directions and preview; do not enter units, a percent sign, or multiple alternatives. [8] [9]

Where to go after a miss

Use the worked-example link to study a specific repair. Use the reference link when the main need is retrieving a formula or condition. Use Section 5 to decide whether the next task should be targeted practice, a changed representation, or a later mixed check.

Practice design and limitations Each original rehearsal has 15 Algebra, 15 Advanced Math, 7 Data Analysis, and 7 Geometry questions, with 33 multiple-choice and 11 numeric-response items. Both modules are fixed; no adaptive routing, undisclosed experimental questions, or scaled-score conversion is simulated. Every one of the 44 questions counts in the rehearsal’s raw review total.

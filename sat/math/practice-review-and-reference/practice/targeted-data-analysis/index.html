Question 1

  • Guide question P-D01
  • Standard

A recipe uses flour and sugar in the ratio 7 : 3. If it uses 420 grams of flour, how many grams of sugar does it use?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 180 The scale factor is 420/7 = 60 grams per ratio part. Sugar is 3 parts, so 3(60) = 180 grams.

Question 2

  • Guide question P-D02
  • Standard

A vehicle travels 18 kilometers in 15 minutes at a constant speed. What is its speed in kilometers per hour?

Why this answer

Answer: A / 72 Fifteen minutes is one-quarter hour. The rate is 18/(1/4) = 72 kilometers per hour.

Rates and units / Standard Review: Example 2.13.

Question 3

  • Guide question P-D03
  • Standard

A price rises from $80 to $92. What is the percent increase?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 15 The increase is 12, measured relative to the original 80. Thus (12/80)(100) = 15%.

Percent change / Standard Review: Example 2.02.

Question 4

  • Guide question P-D04
  • Standard

The dot plot shows seven observations. What is their median?

Why this answer

Answer: B / 4 The ordered observations are 1, 2, 2, 4, 4, 4, 7. The fourth observation, the middle of seven, is 4. Dot height gives frequency, not a separate data value.

One-variable data / Standard Review: Data reference.

Question 5

  • Guide question P-D05
  • Standard

Ten observations have mean 16. One observation of value 38 is added. What is the new mean?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 18 The original total is 10(16) = 160. The new total is 198 across 11 observations, so the new mean is 198/11 = 18.

Means and totals / Standard Review: Example 6.13.

Question 6

  • Guide question P-D06
  • Standard

Every value in a data set is multiplied by 3. Which statement must be true?

Why this answer

Answer: A / The standard deviation is multiplied by 3. Multiplying all values by positive 3 multiplies the mean and every deviation from that mean by 3. Hence the standard deviation also triples.

Spread and transformations / Standard Review: Example 2.11.

A fitted line is yˆ = 2.4x + 11. What is its predicted value of y at x = 15?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 47 Substitute the input: 2.4(15) + 11 = 36 + 11 = 47. This is the model prediction, not a guarantee about an individual observation.

Question 9

  • Guide question P-D09
  • Challenge

A bag contains 5 green and 7 yellow counters. Two are selected without replacement. What is the probability that both are green?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 5/33 The first-green probability is 5/12. After a green is removed, 4 green counters remain among 11 counters. Multiply: (5/12)(4/11) = 20/132 = 5/33.

Probability without replacement / Challenge Review: Example 6.14.

Question 10

  • Guide question P-D10
  • Standard

In a random sample of 200 residents, 74 support a proposal. Using this sample, estimate the number of supporters among 5000 residents.

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 1850 The sample rate is 74/200 = 0.37. Multiply by 5000 to estimate 1850 supporters. Random sampling does not make the estimate an exact count.

Population inference / Standard Review: Example 2.15.

Question 11

  • Guide question P-D11
  • Standard

A sample estimate is 61% with a margin of error of 5 percentage points. What is the lower endpoint of the reported interval, as a percent?

Why this answer

Answer: A / 56 Subtract the margin in percentage points: 61 − 5 = 56. Do not subtract 5% of 61.

Margin of error / Standard Review: Example 5.08.

Question 12

  • Guide question P-D12
  • Standard

A random sample of a town is surveyed about exercise and sleep. An association is found. Which conclusion is justified by the design alone?

Why this answer

Answer: C / The sample can inform an association estimate for the town, but not a causal conclusion.B Random sampling supports generalization to the sampled population, with uncertainty. Without randomized treatment assignment, the association does not establish causation.

Study design / Standard Review: Example 1.13.

Question 1

  • Guide question P-G01
  • Standard

Two intersecting lines form vertical angles measuring (3x + 7)° and (5x − 19)°. What is x?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 13 Vertical angles are equal, so 3x + 7 = 5x − 19. Then 26 = 2x and x = 13. Both angles measure 46°.

Question 2

  • Guide question P-G02
  • Standard

A triangle has angles 35° and 68°. What is the third angle, in degrees?

Why this answer

Answer: D / 77 Triangle angles total 180°, so the missing angle is 180 − 35 − 68 = 77.

Question 3

  • Guide question P-G03
  • Standard

Similar triangles have corresponding sides 6 and 15. The smaller triangle has area 28. What is the area of the larger triangle?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 175 The length factor is 15/6 = 5/2. Area scales by (5/2)2 = 25/4. Thus the larger area is 28(25/4) = 175.

Similarity and scaling / Standard Review: Example 1.14.

  • Standard

A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. What is its hypotenuse?

Why this answer

Answer: D / 15 Use c2 = 92 + 122 = 225, so the positive length is c = 15. This is a scaled 3–4–5 triangle.

Question 5

In a right triangle, the side opposite an acute angle θ has length 8 and the hypotenuse has length 17. What is sin θ?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 8/17 Sine is opposite divided by hypotenuse. The given side lengths already supply the needed ratio 8/17; the third side need not be found.

Question 7

  • Guide question P-G07
  • Standard

A circle has radius 9. A minor arc subtends a central angle of 80°. Its length is kπ. What is k?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 4 The arc is 80/360 = 2/9 of circumference 18π. Thus its length is 4π, and the requested coefficient is 4.

Question 8

  • Guide question P-G08
  • Standard

What is the center of the circle (x + 5)2 + (y − 2)2 = 49?

Why this answer

Answer: B / (−5, 2) Compare with (x − h)2 + (y − k)2 = r2. Here h = −5 and k = 2. The number 49 determines the radius, not the center.

Question 10

  • Standard

A trapezoid has parallel bases of lengths 8 and 14 and perpendicular height 5. What is its area?

Why this answer

Answer: C / 55 The average base length is (8 + 14)/2 = 11. Multiply by perpendicular height 5 to get area 55.

Question 12

  • Guide question P-G12
  • Challenge

Two similar solids have volumes 64 and 216. A corresponding length in the smaller solid is 10. What is the corresponding length in the larger solid?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 15 The volume factor is 216/64 = 27/8. Its cube root is the length factor 3/2. Multiply 10(3/2) = 15.

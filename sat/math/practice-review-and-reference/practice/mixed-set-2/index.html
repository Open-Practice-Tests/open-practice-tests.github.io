Question 16

  • Guide question M16
  • Challenge

The roots of 2x2 − 7x + 3 = 0 are r and s. What is r2 + s2?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 37/4 The root sum is 7/2 and product is 3/2. Use r2 + s2 = (r + s)2 − 2rs = 49/4 − 3 = 37/4.

Root relationships / Challenge Review: Example 6.15.

Question 17

  • Guide question M17
  • Standard

A 30-liter drink mixture is 20% concentrate by volume. Water is added until it is 12% concentrate by volume. Assuming the volumes add, how many liters of water are added?

Why this answer

Answer: A / 20 The concentrate amount remains 0.20(30) = 6 liters. For final total V, 0.12V = 6 gives V = 50. Added water is 50 − 30 = 20 liters.

Percent mixtures / Standard Review: Example 2.06.

Question 19

  • Guide question M19
  • Standard

The shaded region in the graph includes its boundary. Which inequality describes it?

Why this answer

Answer: B / y ≥ −x + 3 The solid boundary is the line through (0, 3) and (3, 0), so its equation is y = −x + 3. The shaded side is above the line, and the solid boundary includes equality.

Graphing inequalities / Standard Review: Example 1.04.

Question 20

  • Guide question M20
  • Challenge

A rectangular prism has dimensions 4, 6, and 9. A similar prism has longest dimension 15. What is its volume?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 1000 The length factor is 15/9 = 5/3. Original volume is 4 · 6 · 9 = 216, so the new volume is 216(5/3)3 = 1000.

Similar-solid volume / Challenge Review: Example 4.11.

If f (x) = 2x2 − 8x + 3, what is the minimum value of f ?

Why this answer

Answer: B / −5 Complete the square: f (x) = 2(x − 2)2 − 5. The positive squared term has minimum zero, so the minimum output is −5.

Question 22

  • Guide question M22
  • Standard

A store must earn at least $420 after a fixed fee of $36 is subtracted from revenue. Each item sells for $19. What is the least number of items it must sell?

Why this answer

Answer: C / 24 The net is 19n − 36 ≥ 420. Thus 19n ≥ 456 and n ≥ 24. Twenty-four gives exactly 420; 23 gives less.

Integer modeling / Standard Review: Example 4.14.

Question 23

  • Guide question M23
  • Standard

A circle has equation x2 + y2 + 4x − 10y = 7. What is its radius?

Why this answer

Answer: C / 6 Completing squares gives (x + 2)2 + (y − 5)2 = 7 + 4 + 25 = 36. The radius is 6, not 36.

Question 24

  • Guide question M24
  • Standard

For the linear function f, f (4) − f (1) = 15. What is f (9) − f (5)?

Why this answer

Answer: B / 20 The slope is 15/(4 − 1) = 5. An input change of 4 therefore changes the output by 5(4) = 20. The intercept cancels.

Answer: A / 1.2 Eight minutes is 480 seconds. Volume is 2.5(480) = 1200 liters, or 1200/1000 = 1.2 m3.

Question 29

  • Guide question M29
  • Standard

If x + 1 = x − 1, what is the solution?

Why this answer

Answer: C / 3 Require x ≥ 1. Squaring gives x + 1 = x2 − 2x + 1, so x(x − 3) = 0. The candidate 0 fails the restriction; 3 gives 2 = 2.

Extraneous solutions / Standard Review: Example 4.08.

Question 30

  • Guide question M30
  • Challenge

Positive numbers a and b satisfy a + b = 12 and ab = 20. What is (a − b)2?

Why this answer

Answer: A / 64 Use (a − b)2 = (a + b)2 − 4ab = 144 − 80 = 64. The actual numbers 2 and 10 verify the result, but need not be found.

Algebraic identities / Challenge Review: Example 6.15.

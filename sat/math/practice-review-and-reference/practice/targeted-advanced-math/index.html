Question 1

  • Guide question P-N01
  • Foundation

Which expression is equivalent to x2 + 9x + 20?

Why this answer

Answer: B / (x + 4)(x + 5) The constant terms must multiply to 20 and add to 9. The pair 4 and 5 works. Expanding verifies x2 + 5x + 4x + 20.

Equivalent expressions / Foundation Review: Example 1.05.

Question 2

  • Guide question P-N02
  • Standard

For x > 0, simplify (x3/4)8.

Why this answer

Answer: D / x6 For a positive base, multiply the exponents: (3/4)(8) = 6. This differs from the addition rule used when multiplying powers with the same base.

Exponents / Standard Review: Example 6.07.

Question 4

  • Guide question P-N04
  • Standard

What is the larger solution to x2 − 11x + 24 = 0?

Why this answer

Answer: A / 8 Factor as (x − 3)(x − 8) = 0. The solutions are 3 and 8, and the larger is 8.

Quadratic equations / Standard Review: Example 6.04.

Question 5

  • Guide question P-N05
  • Standard

For f (x) = 3(x + 2)2 + 5, what is the minimum value of f ?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 5 The squared term is nonnegative and becomes zero at x = −2. Therefore the least output is 5. The vertex input is not the requested minimum value.

Quadratic features / Standard Review: Example 1.07.

Question 6

  • Guide question P-N06
  • Standard

For what value of c does x2 + 10x + c = 0 have exactly one distinct real solution?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 25 The leading coefficient is 1, so set the discriminant to zero: 100 − 4c = 0, giving c = 25. The polynomial is then (x + 5)2.

Discriminants / Standard Review: Example 6.05.

Question 7

  • Guide question P-N07
  • Challenge

Solve x + 10 = x − 2 over the real numbers.

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 6 The right side requires x ≥ 2. Squaring gives x + 10 = x2 − 4x + 4, so (x − 6)(x + 1) = 0. Only 6 satisfies the restriction, and 16 = 4 = 6 − 2.

Radical equations / Challenge Review: Example 1.08.

Question 8

  • Guide question P-N08
  • Standard

For x ≠ −4, which expression is equivalent to x2+2x−8x+4?

Why this answer

Answer: C / x − 2 Factor the numerator as (x + 4)(x − 2) and cancel x + 4, preserving x ≠ −4.

Rational expressions / Standard Review: Example 5.04.

Question 9

  • Guide question P-N09
  • Standard

What is the sum of the solutions to |2x − 3| = 9?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 3 Solve 2x − 3 = 9 and 2x − 3 = −9 to get x = 6 and x = −3. Their sum is 3.

Absolute-value equations / Standard Review: Advanced Math reference.

Question 10

  • Guide question P-N10
  • Standard

A quantity is modeled by Q(t) = 700(0.85)t. By what percent does it decrease when t increases by 1?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 15 The multiplier 0.85 retains 85% of the prior value. The decrease is 100 − 85 = 15%. Enter 15 because the question asks for the percent.

Exponential interpretation / Standard Review: Example 1.09.

Question 11

  • Guide question P-N11
  • Standard

If f (x) = (x − 3)2 and g(x) = f (x) + 4, what is the vertex of the graph of g?

Why this answer

Answer: C / (3, 4) Adding 4 shifts every output up by 4 while leaving the vertex input 3 unchanged. Thus g(x) = (x − 3)2 + 4.

Function transformations / Standard Review: Example 4.06.

Question 12

  • Guide question P-N12
  • Challenge

The graphs y = x2 and y = 3x + 10 intersect. What is the sum of the x-coordinates of their intersections?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

Answer: 3 Equating outputs gives x2 − 3x − 10 = 0, with roots 5 and −2. Their sum is 3; the root-sum relation also gives this directly.

Nonlinear systems / Challenge Review: Example 3.04.

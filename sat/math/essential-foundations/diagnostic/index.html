Question 1

  • Guide question D01

If 3x + 2 = 20, what is the value of 2x?

Why this answer

C; 12. Subtract 2 to get 3x = 18, so x = 6. The target is 2x, not x, and 2(6) = 12. You can also double 3x = 18 to get 6x = 36 and divide by 3. Choice A stops at an intermediate value. Repair: Example 1.01.

Question 2

  • Guide question D02

If 8a = 3, what is the value of a? Write an exact fraction or terminating decimal.

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

3/8 or 0.375. Divide both sides of 8a = 3 by 8. The fraction 3/8 is exact; converting it to a decimal gives 0.375. Both 3/8 and.375 are suitable numeric entries. Reversing the quotient to 8/3 would not satisfy the equation. Check: 8(3/8) = 3. Repair: Example 1.03.

Question 3

  • Guide question D03

A club has 80 members, and 15% of them are new members. How many new members does the club have?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

12 new members. Fifteen percent means 15/100 = 0.15, so the number of new members is 0.15(80) = 12. A mental check is 10% of 80 plus 5% of 80: 8 + 4 = 12. The requested answer is a member count, not the percent number 15. Repair: Example 1.09.

Question 4

  • Guide question D04

What is the value of 7 − 2(3)?

Why this answer

A; 1. Multiplication precedes subtraction: 7 − 2(3) = 7 − 6 = 1. Computing (7 − 2)(3) would instead give 15, but there are no parentheses grouping 7 − 2 in the original expression. Read grouping before applying operations. Repair: Example 2.02.

Question 5

  • Guide question D05

What is the value of −9 + 4(−2)?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

−17. First multiply 4(−2) = −8. Then −9 + (−8) = −17. Adding two negative quantities moves farther below zero; it does not produce a positive sum. A number-line check starts at −9 and moves 8 units left. Repair: Example 2.01.

Question 6

  • Guide question D06

What is the value of 56 − 14?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

7/12. Use a common denominator of 12: 5/6 = 10/12 and 1/4 = 3/12. Subtract the numerators to get (10 − 3)/12 = 7/12. Do not subtract denominators. The result is smaller than 5/6 and positive, as subtracting 1/4 should produce. Repair: Example 2.04.

Question 7

  • Guide question D07

What is the value of 35 ÷ 910?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

2/3.

Division by a nonzero fraction is multiplication by its reciprocal:

35 ÷ 910 = 35 · 109 = 3045 = 23.

Only the divisor is inverted. Because 9/10 is less than 1, the quotient should be greater than 3/5; 2/3 is. Repair: Example 2.07.

Question 8

  • Guide question D08

What is the value of −32 + (−3)2?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

0. The first term is −32 = −(32) = −9. The second is (−3)2 = (−3)(−3) = 9. Their sum is 0. Parentheses determine whether the negative sign belongs to the base that is squared. Repair: Example 2.02.

Question 9

  • Guide question D09

Which expression is equal to 0.0064?

Why this answer

A; 6.4 × 10−3. Moving the decimal in 0.0064 three places to the right produces the coefficient 6.4, so the compensating power is 10−3. Multiplying 6.4 by 0.001 restores 0.0064. A positive exponent would make the value large, not small. Repair: Example 2.12.

Question 10

  • Guide question D10

What is 7 ( 23 ), rounded to the nearest tenth?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

4.7. Keep the fraction exact until the end: 7(2/3) = 14/3 = 4.666.... The hundredths digit is 6, so rounding to the nearest tenth gives 4.7. Rounding 2/3 first can change the final answer. Repair: Example 2.14.

Question 11

  • Guide question D11

Which expression is equivalent to 4(2x − 3) − x?

Why this answer

B; 7x − 12. Distribute 4 to both terms: 4(2x − 3) − x = 8x − 12 − x. Combine the like terms 8x − x = 7x to obtain 7x − 12. The constant stays −12; it is not combined with an x-term. Repair: Example 3.05.

Question 12

  • Guide question D12

If a = −3 and b = 4, what is the value of −2a2 + 3b?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

−6. Substitute with parentheses: −2(−3)2 + 3(4) = −2(9) + 12 = −18 + 12 = −6. The square belongs to a, not to the entire term −2a. A negative coefficient does not disappear when the variable is squared. Repair: Example 3.02.

Question 13

  • Guide question D13

In the expression −4x + 9, what is the coefficient of x?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

−4. The coefficient is the signed number multiplying the variable. In −4x + 9, that number is −4, not 4. The constant term is 9. Reading each term with its preceding sign prevents errors during later combining and factoring. Repair: Example 3.01.

Question 14

  • Guide question D14

Which value of x satisfies 3x + 1 = 10?

Why this answer

B; 3. Subtract 1 from both sides of 3x + 1 = 10 to get 3x = 9, then divide by 3 to get x = 3. Substitution gives 3(3) + 1 = 10. A solution is a value that makes the original equality true. Repair: Example 3.09.

Question 15

  • Guide question D15

For x ≠ 3, which expression is equivalent to x2−9x−3?

Why this answer

B; x + 3, with the original restriction x ≠ 3. Factor the numerator: x2 − 9 = (x − 3)(x + 3). When x ≠ 3, divide out the nonzero factor x − 3 to get x + 3. The simplified expression alone is defined at 3, but the original quotient is not. The restriction stays. Repair: Example 3.08.

Question 16

  • Guide question D16

The function f is defined by f (x) = x2 + 3. What is f (−2)?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

7. The input is −2. Replace every x in f (x) = x2 + 3 with (−2): f (−2) = (−2)2 + 3 = 4 + 3 = 7. Function notation names an output; it does not mean multiplication by a separate number f. Repair: Example 4.03.

Question 18

  • Guide question D18

A line passes through (1, 5) and (4, 11). What is its rate of change, Δy/Δx?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

2 units of output per unit of input. The output increases from 5 to 11 while the input increases from 1 to 4. Thus the rate is (11 − 5)/(4 − 1) = 6/3 = 2. An output difference of 6 alone is not a rate because it occurred over 3 input units. Repair: Example 4.07.

Question 20

  • Guide question D20

A van has 18 passenger seats. The variable n counts occupied passenger seats. Which set describes all possible values of n?

Why this answer

B; integers from 0 through 18, inclusive.

The number of occupied passenger seats is a whole-number count. It cannot be negative or exceed 18. Therefore the domain is {0, 1, 2,..., 18}, not every real number in that interval. A count of 2.5 occupied seats is not permitted by the model. Repair: Example 4.10.

Question 21

  • Guide question D21

Which expression represents seven less than three times x?

Why this answer

C; 3x − 7. Start with “three times x,” which is 3x, then subtract 7 because the result is seven less than that amount. The expression 7 − 3x reverses the comparison. With x = 10, the described amount is seven less than 30, namely 23. Repair: Example 5.01.

Question 22

  • Guide question D22

The ratio of adults to children in a group is 3 : 2. There are 40 people in the group. How many are adults?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

24 adults. The ratio has 3 + 2 = 5 equal parts. Forty people correspond to 5 parts, so each part represents 8 people. Adults account for 3 parts: 3(8) = 24. The adult share is 3/5, not 3/2, of the total. Repair: Example 5.04.

Question 23

  • Guide question D23

A delivery costs a fixed $6 plus $3 per item. With a budget of $33, what is the greatest number of items that can be ordered?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

9 items. Let n be the nonnegative integer number of items. The budget condition is 6 + 3n ≤ 33, so 3n ≤ 27 and n ≤ 9. Nine items cost exactly $33 and ten cost $36, so 9 is the greatest permitted count. Repair: Example 5.10.

Question 24

  • Guide question D24

A robot travels at a constant speed of 45 meters per minute for 8 minutes. How many meters does it travel?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

360 meters.

Multiply speed by time: 45metersminute · 8 minutes = 360 meters.

The minute units cancel, leaving the unit asked for. Dividing 45 by 8 would give the wrong quantity and the wrong units. Repair: Example 5.08.

    Guide question D19

    Do the ordered pairs (0, 1), (1, 2), and (0, 4) define y as a function of x? Explain in one sentence.

    Show model answerHide model answer for question D19

    No; input 0 is paired with both 1 and 4. A function assigns exactly one output to each input in its domain. The pairs (0, 1) and (0, 4) violate that rule. Repeated outputs for different inputs would be allowed; the conflict here is a repeated input with different outputs. Repair: Example 4.09.

    Turn the diagnostic into a study route

    After checking the solutions, count an item as secure only when it is correct and you can explain the method without copying the explanation. This stricter count guides review; it does not change how an actual SAT answer is scored.

    Suggested routing, not a score conversion

    All items in a cluster secure: skim its concepts, attempt selected Standard and Challenge examples, then take its exit check.

    One item not secure: repair that skill and confirm it on a new question; do not infer that the entire chapter is weak.

    Two or more not secure: work through that section in order, focusing first on the earliest missing prerequisite.

    Many correct but slow: compare methods, calculator setup, and reading efficiency before repeating basic instruction.

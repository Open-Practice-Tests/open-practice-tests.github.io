Question 2

  • Guide question E5.2

The ratio of red to blue tiles is 2 : 5. There are 84 tiles total. How many are blue?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

60 blue objects. The ratio 2 : 5 has 7 total parts. Each part represents 84/7 = 12 objects. Blue objects account for 5 parts, so there are 5(12) = 60. The remaining 24 red objects verify both the total and the ratio 24 : 60 = 2 : 5. Repair: Example 5.04.

Question 3

  • Guide question E5.3

An item costs $72 after a 10% discount. What was its original price?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

80 dollars. Let P be the original price. A 10% discount leaves 90% of P, so 0.90P = 72 and P = 72/0.90 = 80. Adding 10% of the sale price would use the wrong base. Check: 10% of $80 is $8, leaving $72. Repair: Example 5.06.

Question 4

  • Guide question E5.4

A walker travels at 72 meters per minute for 35 seconds. How far, in meters, does the walker travel?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

42 meters.

Convert the time before multiplying:

72mmin · 35 s · 1 min60 s = 42 m.

Alternatively, 72 meters per minute is 1.2 meters per second; then 1.2(35) = 42. Repair: Example 5.09.

Question 5

  • Guide question E5.5

A group needs 157 chairs and already has 22. Chairs can be rented only in bundles of 10 for $18 per bundle. What is the minimum rental cost?

Enter an integer, decimal or fraction without units. Use up to 5 characters, or 6 including a leading minus.

Why this answer

252 dollars. The shortage is 157 − 22 = 135 chairs. At 10 chairs per bundle, 135/10 = 13.5 bundles are needed, so buy 14 whole bundles. Their cost is 14(18) = 252 dollars. Thirteen bundles would provide only 22 + 130 = 152 chairs, fewer than required. Repair: Example 5.15.

Guide question E5.1

Write an expression for four less than three times n.

Show model answerHide model answer for question E5.1

3n − 4. Start with three times n, namely 3n, and subtract 4. “Four less than” modifies that amount; it does not mean 4 − 3n. A test value such as n = 10 should produce four less than 30, or 26. Repair: Example 5.01.

After the exit check

Check the comparison base, the meaning of the ratio denominator, the direction of the unit conversion, and the whole-bundle requirement. An answer that fits the arithmetic but not the story is not a complete solution.

Section 5 readiness

Before calculating, you should be able to identify the comparison base, define a variable with units, and explain each term in your model. Whole-item constraints require testing neighboring integers, not blindly rounding to the nearest integer.

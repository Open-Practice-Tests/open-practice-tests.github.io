Question 7

  • Guide question 2.07
  • Foundation

While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:

1. A student team built a model footbridge for a school exhibition.

2. The bridge needed to support a 3-kilogram load without bending visibly.

3. The team tested folded cardboard beams and flat cardboard strips of equal length.

4. Only the folded beams supported the required load without visible bending.

5. The team selected folded beams and painted the completed model green.

The student wants to explain why the team selected folded cardboard beams. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?

Why this answer

Select evidence that explains a design decision | Review lesson 2.1

Correct choice: The team selected folded cardboard beams because they supported the required 3-kilogram load without visible bending, satisfying the model bridge's requirement.

C connects the requirement in note 2 to the successful result in note 4 and the decision in note 5. The key explains the selection; simply describing a controlled test would leave the decisive evidence unstated.

A: Accurate project details, but the color and exhibition do not explain why folded beams met the requirement.

B: Accurate description of the test, but omits its outcome and therefore does not explain the selection.

D: Unsupported: no painting comparison is given, and success with 3 kilograms does not establish success with any load.

If you missed it: Pair a stated requirement with the result that met it, then connect that pair to the decision.

Reading and Sentence Foundations: diagnostic

10 questions in random order. There is no built-in timer or score. Choose or enter an answer, then press Show solution to see how you did and why. Difficulty stays hidden until you open the solution. Reloading starts a fresh, shuffled session.

Work through the set before checking solutions. Note the evidence for each answer and why the nearest alternative fails. Question numbers follow the shuffled order; Guide question labels match the guide references.

Work untimed. Choose one answer per question and mark your confidence. For each choice, underline or copy the smallest phrase that supports it. Do not check explanations until you finish.

Use errors to choose your next step

Two items per chapter provide only a starting signal. Review a chapter when either answer is wrong, uncertain, or unsupported by a clear explanation. Even two correct answers do not demonstrate mastery; the chapter practice tests the skill more broadly.

Check your reasoning

A correct answer is only part of the task. Compare the evidence you used with the explanation. When you chose incorrectly, name the specific error: structure, reference, logical relationship, scope, or word meaning.

Question

  • Guide question D01
  • Foundation

The collection of letters that the archivists recovered from two sealed boxes offers a different account of the expedition.

Which pair gives the subject head and the main verb of the sentence?

Why this answer

1 | Subject and main verb

Correct choice: collection / offers

The main statement is that the collection offers an account. Collection is the head of the subject phrase; offers is its main verb. The relative clause explains which letters are meant.

A: This pair belongs to the embedded relative clause, not the main clause.

C: Letters is inside the of-phrase; it is not the subject of offers.

D: Boxes is the object of from, not the subject of recovered.

If you missed it: Bracket the of-phrase and the that-clause, then read the remaining main statement.

Question

  • Guide question D02
  • Foundation

Although the first survey omitted several smaller settlements, ______

Which choice completes the text so that it forms a complete, grammatical sentence?

Why this answer

1 | Sentence completeness

Correct choice: the researchers included them in a later survey.

Although introduces a dependent clause. The completion must supply a main clause. The researchers included them has a subject and a finite predicate.

A: Including begins a nonfinite phrase; no main clause results.

B: This is a noun phrase without a main finite verb.

C: Included belongs to the dependent which-clause, so the whole text still lacks a main predicate.

If you missed it: Locate the main finite verb outside any dependent clause.

Question

  • Guide question D03
  • Transfer

Although repairs made the old telescope more reliable, they did not increase the amount of light it could gather, which remained limited by the size of its mirror.

Which choice best restates the sentence?

Why this answer

2 | Qualification in a paraphrase

Correct choice: The telescope worked more dependably, but its light-gathering capacity did not increase.

The sentence separates two outcomes: reliability improved, whereas light-gathering capacity did not increase. The mirror still constrained that capacity. The sentence does not specify an exact measurement before and after repair.

B: It changes did not increase into an increase.

C: A limitation caused by size does not mean the size decreased.

D: It reverses the stated improvement and invents a loss of light.

If you missed it: Keep the improvement in reliability and the absence of an increase in light-gathering capacity separate.

Question

  • Guide question D04
  • Transfer

The archive digitized its maps but left the handwritten annotations unsearchable. This omission made it difficult to locate references to minor roads.

What does "This omission" refer to?

Why this answer

2 | Reference tracking

Correct choice: The failure to make the handwritten annotations searchable

The omission is the missing step of making the annotations searchable. Digitization itself did occur.

A: The archive digitized maps; their absence is not stated.

B: Difficulty locating a reference does not establish that it is absent.

D: Searching is the affected activity, not the omitted action.

If you missed it: Replace this omission with a short description of the preceding missing action.

Question

  • Guide question D05
  • Transfer

Several critics describe a novelist's brief chapters as evidence of superficial treatment. Yet the chapters repeatedly revisit the same event from conflicting perspectives, requiring readers to revise their initial judgments.

Which statement best reflects the passage's response to the critics?

Why this answer

3 | Author and cited view

Correct choice: The chapters' brevity can coexist with demanding interpretive work.

The sentence after yet challenges the equation of brief chapters with superficial treatment. Repeated perspective changes require interpretive effort.

A: The passage gives no such preference.

C: It explicitly says the chapters revisit the same event.

D: Revising judgments is inconsistent with an immediate settled interpretation.

If you missed it: Label the first view Critics and the response Passage.

Question

  • Guide question D06
  • Transfer

A survey found that residents who used a neighborhood reading room reported greater satisfaction with local services than residents who did not use it. The survey did not measure their satisfaction before the reading room opened.

Which conclusion is best supported?

Why this answer

3 | Evidence and causal restraint

Correct choice: Use of the reading room was associated with higher reported satisfaction, but the survey does not establish that use caused the difference.

The survey reports an association. Without prior satisfaction or another basis for ruling out competing explanations, the causal claim is not established.

A: It adds causation and every resident.

B: A difference between groups does not imply no exceptions.

C: Failure to establish an effect does not establish no effect.

If you missed it: Write the observed relationship first; then ask what evidence would justify a causal claim.

Question

  • Guide question D07
  • Foundation

Not all of the newly cataloged drawings were signed.

Which statement must be true?

Why this answer

4 | Not all versus none

Correct choice: At least one of the drawings was unsigned.

Not all denies that every drawing was signed. It therefore guarantees at least one unsigned drawing, without specifying the number of signed drawings.

A: Not all does not mean none.

B: The sentence provides no majority.

D: No exact count of signed drawings is supplied.

If you missed it: Translate not all into at least one exception.

Question

  • Guide question D08
  • Transfer

Only proposals that include a maintenance plan are eligible for the grant.

Which statement follows?

Why this answer

4 | Necessary condition

Correct choice: A proposal without a maintenance plan is ineligible.

A maintenance plan is necessary for eligibility. A proposal lacking it cannot be eligible.

B: The condition does not guarantee eligibility or an award.

C: Eligibility is different from receiving a grant.

D: Other requirements may exist.

If you missed it: Read only as imposing a requirement, then keep eligibility separate from selection.

Question

  • Guide question D09
  • Transfer

The curator qualified her praise for the exhibit by noting that its labels sometimes omitted information needed to understand the objects.

As used in the text, "qualified" most nearly means

Why this answer

5 | Meaning in context

Correct choice: limited

She adds a reservation to her praise. Qualified means limited or modified by a condition here.

A: The curator is not earning the praise herself.

C: The criticism does not verify the praise.

D: Adding a reservation is different from repeating praise.

If you missed it: Use the by-phrase to determine what action qualified describes.

Question

  • Guide question D10
  • Transfer

The team welcomed criticism that identified specific weaknesses and suggested feasible repairs. It found such feedback ______ because it could guide the next revision.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?

Why this answer

5 | Precision among related words

Correct choice: constructive

Specific weaknesses, feasible repairs, and guidance for revision point to feedback that helps improve the work: constructive.

A: Feedback that guides revision need not settle an issue conclusively.

B: Deferential means respectfully yielding to someone; usefulness is the focus here.

C: Incidental means secondary or occurring as a minor accompaniment, which conflicts with its stated guiding role.

If you missed it: Predict a plain phrase such as useful for improvement before comparing choices.

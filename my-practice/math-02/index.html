    Math, Module 1 · Question 1

    Which expression is equivalent to (x + 4)2 − (x − 4)2 + 3x?

    1. 16x

    2. 19x

    3. 2x2 + 19x

    4. 3x

    Correct answer: B

    Worked solution

    Answer: B. 19x Use a difference of squares: [(x + 4) − (x − 4)][(x + 4) + (x − 4)] = 8(2x) = 16x. Adding 3x gives 19x. Direct expansion gives the same result, but requires more terms.

    Watch for: The constant square terms cancel; the middle terms add rather than cancel.

    x−214

    Worked solution

    Math, Module 1 · Question 3

    Two adjacent interior angles of a parallelogram measure (7x + 8)° and (5x + 16)°. What is the positive difference between their measures, in degrees?

    1. 81

    2. 18

    3. 24

    4. 13

    Correct answer: B

    Worked solution

    Answer: B. 18 Adjacent angles in a parallelogram are supplementary, so 7x + 8 + 5x + 16 = 180. This gives 12x = 156 and x = 13. The angles measure 99° and 81°, whose difference is 18°.

    Math, Module 1 · Question 8

    The histogram represents ten integer measurements. Each interval includes its left endpoint but not its right endpoint. What is the greatest possible mean of the ten measurements?

    1. 34.5

    2. 39

    3. 40

    4. 30

    Correct answer: B

    Worked solution

    Answer: B. 39 The three bins contain 3, 4, and 3 measurements. To maximize the mean, place every measurement at the largest integer in its bin: 29, 39, or 49. The greatest possible sum is 3(29) + 4(39) + 3(49) = 390, so the greatest mean is 39.

    Watch for: The right endpoints 30, 40, and 50 belong to the next intervals and cannot be used as values in these bins.

    Math, Module 1 · Question 14

    A laboratory performs two calibration procedures. Three runs of procedure A and two runs of procedure B take 146 minutes in total. Five runs of A and three runs of B take 237 minutes. Each procedure always takes the same amount of time. How many more minutes does one run of A take than one run of B?

    Correct answer: 17

    Worked solution

    Answer: 17 Let a and b be the run times. The equations are 3a + 2b = 146 and 5a + 3b = 237. Multiply the first by 3 and the second by 2: 9a + 6b = 438 and 10a + 6b = 474. Subtract to obtain a = 36. Then 108 + 2b = 146, so b = 19. The difference is 17.

    Watch for: The totals refer to multiple runs; do not compare the totals directly.

    Math, Module 1 · Question 17

    Researchers recruited 120 volunteers and randomly assigned each to solve the same puzzle using layout A or layout B. Conditions other than layout were kept the same. The analysis found a difference in average completion time unlikely to be explained by chance assignment alone. Which conclusion is best supported?

    1. The faster layout must help every individual participant.

    2. The results establish the exact average effect for all adults.

    3. The experiment provides evidence that layout affected average completion time for these participants under the tested conditions.

    4. No causal conclusion is possible because the participants volunteered.

    Correct answer: C

    Worked solution

    Answer: C. The experiment provides evidence that layout affected average completion time for these participants under the tested conditions. Random assignment helps isolate the effect of layout within the experiment. The stated analysis supports an effect on average completion time under these conditions. Because the volunteers were not randomly sampled from all adults, broad population generalization is not established by the design alone.

    Watch for: Random assignment supports a causal comparison; it does not make a volunteer group representative of every adult.

    Math, Module 1 · Question 20

    A function has the form f (x) = abx + c, where a > 0 and b > 1. If f (0) = 7, f (1) = 11, and f (2) = 19, what is f (−1)?

    Math, Module 2 · Question 3

    A solid is enlarged by the same scale factor in every dimension. Its surface area increases by 44%, and its volume increases by 91 cubic units. What was its original volume, in cubic units?

    Correct answer: 125

    Worked solution

    Answer: 125 The surface-area multiplier is 1.44, so the length multiplier is 1.44 = 1.2. The volume multiplier is 1.23 = 1.728. If the original volume is V, the increase is 1.728V − V = 0.728V = 91. Therefore V = 91/0.728 = 125. The enlarged volume is 216, which is 91 more than 125.

    Watch for: A 44% increase in surface area is neither a 44% increase in length nor a 44% increase in volume.

    Math, Module 2 · Question 21

    For exactly two real values of a, the system (a + 2)x + 4y = 6 and 6x + (a − 2)y = 9 has no solution. What is the sum of the squares of those two values of a?

    1. 112

    2. 56

    Math, Module 1 · Question 1

    A print shop charges a fixed setup fee plus a constant price per poster. An order of 8 posters costs $91, and adding 5 posters to any order increases the charge by $32.50. What is the charge for 12 posters?

    1. $123.50

    2. $169

    3. $104

    4. $117

    Correct answer: D

    Worked solution

    Answer: D. $117 The price per poster is 32.50/5 = 6.50 dollars. Moving from 8 to 12 posters adds 4 posters, so the charge is 91 + 4(6.50) = 117 dollars. The setup fee is already included in the $91; it is not charged again.

    Watch for: Do not add the cost of five posters when the order increases by only four.

    Math, Module 1 · Question 2

    For every real value of x, which number is equal to 9x+132x−1?

    1. 3

    2. 81

    3. 9

    4. 27

    Correct answer: D

    Worked solution

    Answer: D. 27 Write 9 as 32. The quotient is 32x+2/32x−1 = 3(2x+2)−(2x−1) = 33 = 27. The variable terms cancel, so the expression is constant.

    Watch for: Subtract the entire denominator exponent, including its negative constant.

    Math, Module 1 · Question 4

    In triangle ABC, side BC is extended through C to point D. Ray CE bisects exterior angle ACD, and angle ACE measures 67°. Angle A is 22° greater than angle B. What is the measure of angle B, in degrees?

    1. 78

    2. 56

    3. 45

    4. 67

    Correct answer: B

    Worked solution

    Answer: B. 56 Because CE bisects the exterior angle, angle ACD measures 2(67) = 134°. The exterior-angle theorem gives A + B = 134°. With A = B + 22°, this becomes 2B + 22 = 134, so B = 56°. The other angles are A = 78° and C = 46°, which sum with B to 180°.

    Watch for: Double the bisected exterior angle before applying the exterior-angle theorem.

    2. −12

    3. 12

    4. 3

    Correct answer: B

    Worked solution

    Math, Module 1 · Question 9

    What is the sum of all real solutions of |2x − 9| = x + 3?

    Correct answer: 14

    Worked solution

    Answer: 14 For 2x − 9 ≥ 0, solve 2x − 9 = x + 3, obtaining x = 12. For 2x − 9 < 0, solve 9 − 2x = x + 3, obtaining x = 2. Both satisfy their branch conditions and the original equation. Their sum is 14.

    Watch for: Check branch conditions and the original equation; the right side of an absolute-value equation must be nonnegative.

    Math, Module 1 · Question 11

    Two parallel chords of a circle lie on opposite sides of its center. Their lengths are 16 and 30, and the perpendicular distance between the chords is 23. What is the radius of the circle?

    1. 23

    2. 15

    3. 17

    4. 34

    Correct answer: C

    Worked solution

    Answer: C. 17 Let the distances from the center to the shorter and longer chords be a and b. Then a +b = 23. Perpendiculars from the center bisect the chords, so r2 = a2 + 82 = b2 + 152. Hence a2 − b2 = 161, and (a − b)(a + b) = 161 gives a − b = 7. Thus a = 15, b = 8, and r2 = 152 + 82 = 289, so r = 17.

    Watch for: The chord lengths must first be halved. Opposite sides of the center make the two perpendicular distances add to 23.

    Answer: D. 3 Complete the square: (x − 7)2 = 49 − c. Two distinct real roots require c < 49. The smaller root is 7 − 49 − c, which must exceed 5. Thus 49 − c < 2, giving c > 45. The integers are 46, 47, 48, so there are 3.

    Watch for: Both endpoints are excluded: c = 45 gives a root equal to 5, and c = 49 gives a double root.
